# All Things on Quantitative Trading

A structured notes repository for quantitative trading, bringing together market structure, systematic investing, macro, execution, and supporting mathematics in one place.

The goal is to keep the notes practical, source-aware, and concise: books are integrated into the relevant sections rather than summarized chapter by chapter.
