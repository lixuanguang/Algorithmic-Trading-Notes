\noindent Pathways drawn on: \texttt{market-making-lob} (draft entries from the migration: supplemental inventory, information and quoting models, Glosten--Milgrom, a practitioner account of dealer quoting, and textbook accounts of inventory-based quoting; of the pathway's required readings only Avellaneda--Stoikov and Glosten--Milgrom, through its 1983 working paper, are drawn on); and the section overview \texttt{execution-market-making} (draft entries from the migration, from Harris, Hasbrouck and Lehalle and Laruelle; none of its readings yet studied); and \texttt{hft-market-design} (draft entries from the migration: high-frequency trading as a set of strategies sharing speed tools, from the SEC's 2010 concept release and Lehalle and Laruelle; the latency-arbitrage arms race, from Budish, Cramton and Shim, a required reading checked in its published version; and a stylised model in which a narrower spread need not lower other traders' costs, from Lehalle and Laruelle; none of the pathway's readings yet studied). The section beginning with \hyperref[def:fundamental-value]{fundamental value} covers informed trading, informative prices, informed-trader types, the winner's curse, market efficiency, inside information and its deterrence, and the price formation process, from Harris and Lehalle and Laruelle; the section beginning with \hyperref[def:arbitrage]{arbitrage} covers arbitrageurs, order anticipators and bluffers, from Harris. The section beginning with \hyperref[def:spread-measures]{spread measures} measures and decomposes spreads, from Harris and Hasbrouck (the latter a required reading of this section's overview route, not yet studied); the section beginning with \hyperref[def:volatility-components]{fundamental and transitory volatility} covers volatility, returns by time scale, the relation of volatility to spreads, and bubbles and crashes, from Harris, Hasbrouck and Lehalle and Laruelle. A later section compares floor and automated trading systems, from Harris (2002). The last section, for \texttt{fx-dealer-market-making}, covers spot FX market structure, internalisation and last look, from the BIS Markets Committee's 2020 report, the FX Global Code, a provider disclosure and three of the pathway's readings; none of its readings yet studied. The section after it, for \texttt{options-execution}, covers option market-maker spreads, the measurement of option execution costs and retail option order routing, from six of the pathway's readings, one read in full and five through their abstracts; none yet studied. Notes for this section that predate the study method are in Chapter~\ref{legacy:execution-market-making}, in the part \hyperref[legacy]{Notes awaiting migration}.
