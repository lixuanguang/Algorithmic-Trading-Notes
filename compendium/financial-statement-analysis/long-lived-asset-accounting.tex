\section{Long-lived asset accounting}

\begin{qfmethod}[Purchased, internally developed and acquired intangible assets]\label{meth:purchased-developed-acquired-intangibles}
\qffield{Statement} A separately purchased patent normally enters at cost; an identifiable patent acquired with a business enters at acquisition-date fair value separately from goodwill. Internally generated brands, customer lists and goodwill are not recognized under IAS 38. Research is expensed. Development becomes an asset only after demonstrating technical feasibility, intent and ability to complete/use/sell, probable benefits, sufficient resources and reliable cost measurement; earlier expense is not revived merely because the project later succeeds \citep[IAS~38 paras.~21, 33, 48, 54, 57 and~63]{adopted-accounting-standards-2023}.
\qffield{Practice} A purchased license of 60 with a five-year useful life incurs annual amortization 12. An internally developed brand with the same estimated commercial value may carry zero. A business acquisition can recognize a customer relationship absent from the acquiree's accounts. Consequently, two economically similar firms can have different assets, amortization, margins and ROA depending on how they obtained the intangible. Compare after-tax earnings and invested capital on an explicit basis, rather than automatically rewarding the firm with fewer recognized assets.
\qffield{Conditions} US non-software R\&D generally remains expensed; internal-use software and software to be sold follow specialized capitalization rules. ASU 2025-06 changes internal-use software's stage-based test to management commitment plus probable completion/use, with attention to significant development uncertainty. It is effective for annual periods beginning after 15 December 2027, early adoption permitted, and permits prospective, modified or retrospective transition. It does not amend external-use software guidance. A 2026 model without early adoption must not silently use the future test \citep[Summary pp.~1--4]{us-software-2025}; \citep[chap.~8, solutions~1--8, PDF p.~201]{statement-analysis-workbook-2020}.
\qffield{Source scope} ASC 730-10-25-1, reprinted in the issued 2023 amendment, retains expense recognition for R\&D within its scope; acquisition and joint-venture exceptions are distinct from internally incurred non-software development \citep[ASC~730-10-25-1, pp.~37--38]{us-research-2023}.
\end{qfmethod}

\begin{qfmethod}[Impairment, derecognition and long-lived asset disclosures]\label{meth:asset-impairment-disposal-analysis}
\qffield{Statement} Under IAS 36, impairment arises when carrying amount exceeds recoverable amount, the greater of value in use and fair value less disposal costs. Test at the appropriate cash-generating unit when independent asset cash flows cannot be identified. Indefinite-lived intangibles and goodwill have annual testing obligations; other assets are tested when indicators arise. Non-goodwill reversals are limited to the carrying value that would have existed without impairment. US held-and-used finite-lived assets first fail an undiscounted-cash-flow recoverability screen, then are written to fair value; subsequent reversals are prohibited. Held-for-sale and goodwill rules are separate \citep[IAS~36 paras.~9--10, 18, 59, 110, 117 and~124]{adopted-accounting-standards-2023}; \citep[chap.~8, solutions~24--32, PDF pp.~203--204]{statement-analysis-workbook-2020}.
\qffield{Practice} Carrying amount 100, value in use 82 and fair value less disposal costs 75 yield IFRS impairment 18. If US undiscounted cash flows are 110, the held-and-used asset is not impaired by that screen; if they are 95 and fair value is 80, US loss is 20. These differences affect equity, future depreciation and apparent returns on a smaller asset base, but not current cash by themselves. After the IFRS impairment, a sale for 90 gives gain 8 and cash investing inflow 90; the indirect CFO reconciliation subtracts the gain, not the whole proceeds, from net income. Tax is ignored in this isolated comparison.
\qffield{Analysis} Meridian's base PP\&E reconciles $400+80-50=430$. Require analogous class-level bridges including disposals, acquisitions, FX, impairment and revaluation, with useful lives and depreciation methods. A low depreciation/capex ratio may signal expansion or under-depreciation; inspect asset age and capacity first. Upward IFRS revaluation generally creates OCI/equity, except when reversing a prior expense; it must not be labeled operating revenue. Disclosed impairment assumptions and discount-rate sensitivities can expose fragility before a charge appears \citep[IAS~16 paras.~30--31, 39, 67--68, 73; IAS~38 para.~118; IAS~36 para.~130]{adopted-accounting-standards-2023}.
\qffield{Source scope} The US recoverability screen and prohibition of restoration originate in SFAS 144, now superseded as an authoritative document by the Codification. Its historical text supports the mechanism, corroborated by the 2020 workbook; it is not cited as a current consolidated standard \citep[paras.~7--8 and~15, pp.~9--11]{us-impairment-2001}.
\end{qfmethod}
