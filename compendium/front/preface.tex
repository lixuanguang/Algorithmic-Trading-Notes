\chapter{Preface}

This compendium follows the sections of the institutional quantitative finance learning guide, pinned in \texttt{guide/}: one chapter per guide section, holding typed, labelled entries that cite their sources at each claim. Notes written before these notes followed the guide's study method were compiled for a time in a final part, \emph{Notes awaiting migration}; all of them have since been rewritten as entries in their sections' chapters, and their original text is kept outside the compendium, in the repository's \texttt{notes/artefacts/}.

This content builds upon the foundational works of Rishi K. Narang \citep{s7-narang}, Raja Velu \citep{s3-velu}, and Marcos L\'opez de Prado \citep{alpha-afml}, among others, whose insights form the backbone of our discussion.
