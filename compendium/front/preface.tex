\chapter{Preface}

This compendium follows the sections of the institutional quantitative finance learning guide, pinned in \texttt{guide/}: one chapter per guide section, holding typed, labelled entries that cite their sources at each claim. Notes written before these notes followed the guide's study method are compiled in the final part, \hyperref[legacy]{Notes awaiting migration}, until each is rewritten as entries in its section's chapter.

This content builds upon the foundational works of Rishi K. Narang \citep{s7-narang}, Raja Velu \citep{s3-velu}, and Marcos L\'opez de Prado \citep{alpha-afml}, among others, whose insights form the backbone of our discussion.
