\chapter*{Notation and conventions}\label{drv:notation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation and conventions}

Notation follows the compendium entries each derivation serves; the compendium's general notation list applies otherwise. Prices are in currency per unit, quantities in units (shares), and variances of one period's shock in squared price units, not annualised. The symbols below are local to a model, and some are reused with different meanings across models.

Each derivation states the assumptions under which its results hold and names its sources with locators. The working is own derivation following the cited textbook accounts; it records source checking, not study.
