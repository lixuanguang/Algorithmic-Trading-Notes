\begin{model} \hlt{Specialist Participation Option}\\
Let $V_{\mathrm{step}}$ be the expected value of stepping ahead or trading with the book, $C_{\mathrm{obligation}}$ the expected loss from required liquidity provision, and $K$ the compliance and capital cost. A specialist assignment is privately attractive when $V_{\mathrm{step}}>C_{\mathrm{obligation}}+K$.
Regulators should ask the public version: whether the price-continuity benefit exceeds the rents transferred through the privilege.
\end{model}

% assign: execution-market-making
\begin{caveat} \hlt{Tick Size and Participation}\\
Small ticks reduce the cost of stepping ahead of public orders, making specialist or DMM participation easier and public queue value weaker. Larger ticks protect queue rents but can widen quoted spreads.
\end{caveat}
