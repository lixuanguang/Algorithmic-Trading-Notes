\subsection*{Fixed Income Microstructure}

\begin{taxonomy} \hlt{Fixed-Income Instrument Range}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*.]
\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item \hlt{government bonds}: most standardised and electronically tradable;
\item \hlt{corporate bonds}: fragmented by issuer, maturity, covenant, and liquidity;
\item \hlt{credit derivatives}: standard indices trade more centrally than bespoke risk;
\item \hlt{swaps and structured products}: often remain dealer-mediated because contracts differ.
\end{enumerate}
\end{taxonomy}

\begin{principle} \hlt{RFQ Dominance}\\
Many fixed-income products trade through RFQ because each bond or swap line is less standardised and less continuously active than an equity. Dealers compete by quote, balance sheet, inventory, and relationship rather than only queue position.
\end{principle}

\begin{mechanism} \hlt{Dealer Balance Sheet as Venue}\\
For less liquid bonds, the dealer's inventory and willingness to warehouse risk are part of the market structure. Liquidity is therefore partly bilateral and may disappear when balance-sheet cost rises.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{marketrule} \hlt{TRACE and Post-Trade Transparency}\\
TRACE reports U.S. corporate bond trades after execution, improving post-trade transparency even when pre-trade negotiation remains bilateral. Similar transparency pushes in Europe aim to make fixed-income liquidity easier to measure without forcing all products into a CLOB.
\end{marketrule}

\begin{caveat} \hlt{Pre-Trade Transparency Limits}\\
Publishing too much pre-trade interest in illiquid bonds can reveal inventory needs and increase dealer risk. Fixed-income transparency policy must balance market monitoring against the cost of exposing fragile liquidity.
\end{caveat}

\begin{practitionernote} \hlt{Data Changes the Market}\\
Richer order, quote, and trade records let asset managers measure transaction costs, compare dealers, and link investment decisions to liquidity. Electronification changes execution quality and governance of trading desks.
\end{practitionernote}
