## Key results

- Spread = basket price -/+ C/R fee per creation unit, slippage margin and expected financing (p. 140).
- $2,000 creation fee on 100,000 shares = 2 cents; fees embedded in spreads; the redemption example prints 200,000/$2,000 for 2,000/200,000 (p. 48).
- Clients do not create or redeem; APs use the process to flatten positions and offer NAV executions in any size (pp. 141-142).
- NAV premiums of domestic ETFs are 'marketplace noise', from late trading and wider closing spreads; international ETFs trade on expected values (p. 214).
- 2x leveraged fund: +10% for ten days gives +159% and +519%; -10% gives 34.87 and 10.74 (pp. 269-270).
- ETNs are senior unsecured debt backed only by the issuer's credit (p. 283); asset-weighted expense ratio 30 bp in July 2015 (p. 30).
