## Scope, dates and limitations

EU-adopted IFRS text, not an IASB-issued or current consolidated edition. Individual standards have their own effective dates; all ordinary recognition rules used apply in the stated 2026 public-company cases. IAS1 para69 in this original text is superseded by the 2024 classification/covenant amendments; do not reuse its unconditional-right wording. Separately identified IFRS9/7 amendments apply from 2026 and IFRS18 from 2027. Read only the paragraphs listed below, with intervening text where needed, not the entire annex.

The worked company cases and numerical assumptions are original. Source inspection supports the stated mechanisms, not a claim that these synthetic forecasts were observed. No unlisted annex or original empirical paper is claimed reviewed.
