## Key results

- Model (abstract): short selling by searching for lenders and bargaining over the fee; hard-to-locate securities have initially elevated prices expected to decline, possibly above the most optimistic buyer's valuation because of lending fees.
- Institutions (sec. 2, U.S., 2001): locate from broker inventory, lending customers, custodians, institutions; partial fills and delays; pay-for-hold (pp. 5-6); same-day loan, broker as principal both sides, 102% (105% international) cash collateral, rebate r/360 daily, broker rebate spread, non-cash collateral with outright fee rare (1% of one custodian's loans) (p. 7); Reg T additional 50% for retail, retail receive no rebate or lending fee (p. 7, fn. 9); open loans, recall, replacement borrow, buy-in, cash-in-lieu of dividends, voting rights pass to borrower (p. 8).
