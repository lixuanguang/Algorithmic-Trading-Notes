## Key results

- Securities lending, like repo, is a securities financing transaction; legal title passes against collateral (which may also be pledged; uncollateralised loans exist).
- Collateral cash or securities; borrower pays a fee; with cash collateral the lender reinvests and rebates part of the return, deducting the fee, so the fee is implicit in the rebate rate.
- Repo mostly bond collateral; an important segment of lending is equities; lending of equity transfers voting rights and corporate actions, hence the convention of an express right of recall; repo normally has no recall unless agreed.
- Repo mostly cash-driven, lending mostly security-driven; overlap with the specials segment of repo. GMSLA (ISLA) and GMRA (ICMA) as model contracts.
