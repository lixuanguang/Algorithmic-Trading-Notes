## Key results

- The handbook does not override contracts; read with the GMSLA, which prevails; local codes of conduct prevail over it (pp. 2-3).
- Cash collateral by DVP or FOP (FOP most common for EMEA equity lending); rebate a negotiated spread in basis points over or under a benchmark (pp. 7-8, updated 15 January 2021).
- Recall flow: notice with ISIN, quantity, settlement date, at latest one hour before market close minus two business days or the settlement cycle if greater; ACK/NACK; on failure, costs claimed under GMSLA 9.3, paid within one business day, loan closed and collateral returned (pp. 24-25, updated 28 January 2022).
- Partial returns and recalls accepted; sale-fail costs only for the failing portion; CSDR claims within 30 days (pp. 23 and 26).
