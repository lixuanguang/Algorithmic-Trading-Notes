## Key results

- Pp. 1-2, 11-19: debt is a fixed claim and equity a residual one; stakeholders' conflicts; board weaknesses; ownership devices (dual class, pyramids, cross-holdings, golden shares, pp. 16-17, 23-24).
- Pp. 25-27, 38-45: governance-index evidence (Gompers-Ishii-Metrick 8.5 percent a year); risk shifting, added debt and dividends transfer wealth from lenders (Nabisco bonds about -19 percent); lender protections; activism; social costs.
- Pp. 88-148: risk-free rate by currency; survey, historical and implied premiums and when to use each (pp. 94-105); bottom-up betas and Hamada relevering (pp. 116-123); total beta for private firms (Bookscape 0.8558/0.5099 = 1.6783, pp. 135-136); cost of debt from bonds, ratings and synthetic ratings (pp. 137-141); market-value weights (pp. 146-148).
- Pp. 160-270: incremental cash flows, sunk and allocated costs, NPV and IRR rules and conflicts, multiple IRRs, rationing and the profitability index, equivalent annuities, opportunity costs, cannibalisation, real options (delay, expand, abandon), post-audits.
- Pp. 285-331: equity and preferred features; IPO, seasoned and rights issues (pp. 302-309); tax and discipline benefits and distress, agency and flexibility costs of debt; MM with and without taxes; Miller's personal-tax formula (pp. 323-324); pecking order.
- Pp. 343-385, 411-413: cost-of-capital approach (Disney WACC 7.81 percent); APV; comparison with peers and regressions; matching financing to assets.
- Pp. 437-485: dividend chronology, types, payout and yield trends, Lintner smoothing, MM irrelevance, tax systems (imputation, split rate), clienteles, signals, agency; buyback effects and methods (p. 473-474), trends (p. 475), FCFE and the cash-returned ratio (pp. 480-485).
