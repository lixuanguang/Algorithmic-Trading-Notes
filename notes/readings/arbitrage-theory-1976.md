## Key results

- pp. 341-343: the CAPM relation (1); factor model (2); four-step heuristic (zero wealth, well diversified, no systematic risk, so the certain return must be zero); E_i = rho + lambda beta_i, eqs. (4)-(5); holds in all but profound disequilibria; "the market portfolio plays no special role".
- pp. 344-346: counterexample with one CARA agent; eq. (9) writes expected CARA utility of normal returns as an exponential of mean and variance; two readings of a growing market (primed and unprimed slot machines).
- pp. 346-347: Type B agents (relative risk aversion uniformly bounded); factor model (15) with only bounded residual variance assumed; Theorem I: the sum over assets of squared deviations from rho + beta_i gamma is bounded, eq. (18).
- p. 353: eq. (24), an asset's premium as the beta-weighted sum of factor premia E^k - rho. pp. 353-354: rough 1962-1971 bound, average absolute discrepancy below 0.00084, about 1 percent of the 7.4 percent market return. p. 355: agents may disagree about the factors' distributions if they agree on expectations and sensitivities.
