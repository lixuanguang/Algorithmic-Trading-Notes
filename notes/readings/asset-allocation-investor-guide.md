## Key results

- p. 1: time horizon; risk tolerance as "your ability and willingness to lose some or all of your original investment".
- p. 2: stocks have the greatest risk and highest returns of the three and are very risky in the short term; bonds are less volatile with more modest returns, high-yield bonds riskier; cash equivalents are safest with the lowest return, their principal concern inflation risk; other categories (real estate, precious metals and other commodities, private equity) have category-specific risks.
- pp. 2-3: asset categories that move differently reduce the risk of loss.
