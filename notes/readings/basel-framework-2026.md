## Key results

- CET1 at least 4.5%, Tier 1 at least 6%, total capital at least 8.0% of RWA at all times (RBC20.1); conservation buffer of 2.5% of CET1 above the minimum, constraining distributions (RBC30.2).
- Leverage ratio = Tier 1 / exposure measure (LEV20.3), at least 3% (LEV20.7).
- LCR: HQLA over total net cash outflows over 30 calendar days of stress, no lower than 100% absent stress (LCR20.4-20.5); NSFR: available over required stable funding, at least 100% (NSF20.2).

## Limits

Committee standards, not national rules; G-SIB and countercyclical buffers and output floors were not read.
