## Key results

- DTS: R = -D s (ds/s); 5% at 80 bp and 3 years equals 3% at 50 bp and 8 years (pp. 3-4).
- LCS = OASD x bid-ask spread; US IG index LCS 0.59%-3.40% from January 2007 to June 2009 (p. 82).
- OAS = expected default loss + expected liquidity cost + risk premium (pp. 133-134).
- Issuer limits: 9.2 : 3.1 : 1 (downgrade only) and 4.0 : 2.5 : 1 (all non-systematic risk) before the crisis; full sample printed '2:6 : 1.5 : 1.0' (2.6) and 2.4 : 1.5 : 1.0; DTS caps respond faster but can force selling (pp. 257-258).
- DTS-sized, issuer-matched credit steepeners earn carry with lower volatility (pp. 213-214); CDS-based replication cheap but with volatile basis tracking error (pp. 337-338).

## Limits

Research of a sell-side group; data to 2010-2011; one typographical error in the position-size ratios.
