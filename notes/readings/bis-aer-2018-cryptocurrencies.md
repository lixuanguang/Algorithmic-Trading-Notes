## Key results

- Cryptocurrencies combine a protocol, a ledger and a decentralised network; permissioned and permissionless DLT differ in who may update.
- Limits: energy use, scalability, congestion fees, unstable value, probabilistic finality and forks.
- DLT may help in niche uses such as trade finance and cross-border payments without a cryptocurrency.
