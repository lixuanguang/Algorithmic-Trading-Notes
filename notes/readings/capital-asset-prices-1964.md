## Key results

- p. 430, Fig. 3: E and sigma of a two-plan combination with correlation r_ab. The diagrams put sigma on the vertical and E on the horizontal axis.
- p. 432, Fig. 4: lending at the pure rate gives points on a line from P; one plan dominates, at the tangency of a ray from P. p. 433: borrowing extends the line; with equal borrowing and lending rates choice is dichotomised (risky combination, then borrow or lend); n. 15 credits Tobin and Hicks. Without borrowing, or with limited borrowing, the assumption is "less onerous than it might initially appear".
- pp. 433-434: equilibrium assumptions: a common pure rate with borrowing or lending on equal terms, and homogeneous expectations; "highly restrictive and undoubtedly unrealistic".
- p. 435: prices adjust until every asset enters at least one combination on the capital market line; n. 19: several efficient combinations, perfectly correlated, against Tobin's uniqueness.
- pp. 437-440, Figs. 7-9 and nn. 22-25: tangency of the asset-combination curve with the CML; regression of R_i on R_g; systematic and unsystematic components; the correlated part "cannot be diversified away"; B_ig linearly related to expected return; n. 23 "diagonal model".
