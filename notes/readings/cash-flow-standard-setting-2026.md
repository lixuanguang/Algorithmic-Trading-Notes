## Key results

- AP20A (2025): the targeted-improvements project covered financial institutions and a disclosure of cash interest received; investors at a 2025 symposium asked to "consider whether all interest should remain classified as operating" (slide 4); the indirect method is widely used, with no calls for a full move to the direct method.
- AP35 (2026): the financial-institutions project was removed from the technical agenda on 22 April 2026 (slide 2); suggested targeted improvements include a cash-interest-received disclosure, removing the word "encouraged" for the direct method, a standard free-cash-flow measure and, among other feedback, presenting interest cash flows "not solely in the operating section" (slides 3-4); next steps are further research (slide 6).

## Limits

Staff papers, not board positions; they show that the interest classification was still under discussion, which implies but does not quote the current requirement. A 2026 proposed Update on cash equivalents was seen only in a search summary and not read.
