## Key results

- 230-10-45-28: entities using the indirect method determine and report the same net operating cash flow indirectly, adjusting net income for the effects of deferrals of past and accruals of expected future operating receipts and payments, and removing items that do not affect operating cash flow.
- 230-10-45-29: the reconciliation of net income to net operating cash flow is provided whether the direct or the indirect method is used.
- 230-10-45-30: under the direct method, the reconciliation is provided in a separate schedule.
