## Key results

- Internalise against externalise (p. 1); internalisation horizon by Little's law proportional to risk limit over flow rate; skewing costs, including information leakage or signalling (p. 2).
- Proposition 1: $W=(R/\lambda_0)c_R/4$ (binary skew), $W\approx(R/\lambda_0)c_R/\sqrt{2\pi}$ (exponential) (p. 8).
- EUR/USD: $R=\$12.75$mn, $\lambda_0=\$3.66$mn/min, $W\approx1.39$ minutes (p. 11, recomputed); single-digit minutes in liquid G10, tens of minutes in many Asian and EM currencies (p. 11).
