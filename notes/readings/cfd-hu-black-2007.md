## Key results

- Decoupling by share lending: the borrower acquires votes without economic ownership; the lender keeps economic ownership without votes; loans callable by the lender, repayable by the borrower (pp. 1, 5).
- Record date capture: borrowing shortly before a meeting's record date and returning soon after; omit the short sale and borrowing becomes empty voting; Regulation T said to bar lending for voting purposes in many circumstances (p. 5).
- Footnote 6: a put and call with the same exercise price and expiration are economically equivalent to selling shares (p. 5).
- Swap-based decoupling (long shares, short swap; long swap) is a separate route (p. 1).
