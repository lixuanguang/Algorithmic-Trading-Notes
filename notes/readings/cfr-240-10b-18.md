## Key results

- (b): safe-harbour conditions: one broker or dealer a day; no opening purchase and none in the last 10 minutes (actively traded) or 30 minutes; price no higher than the highest independent bid or last independent trade; volume no more than 25 percent of average daily trading volume, with a weekly block exception.
- (c): relaxed volume after a market-wide trading suspension. (d): no presumption of manipulation if the conditions are not met.
