## Key results

- Para. (b)(4)(i): a payment toward the minimum required contribution made after the valuation date is discounted to the valuation date at the plan's effective interest rate.
- Para. (b)(4)(ii): to the extent a contribution is a late required installment, it is first discounted from the payment date to the installment's due date at the effective rate plus 5 percentage points, then treated as paid on the due date for the (b)(4)(i) adjustment.
- Para. (c)(1): a plan with a funding shortfall for the preceding plan year must pay quarterly installments; (c)(5): each is 25 percent of the required annual payment, the lesser of 90 percent of the current year's minimum and 100 percent of the preceding year's; (c)(6): due on the 15th day of the 4th, 7th and 10th plan months and 15 days after the plan year.
- Used to label Harlow's 165.12m on 15 September 2027 as the ordinary-interest equivalent of the 151.91m valuation-date minimum, not an amount that would satisfy it.

## Limits

The liquidity requirement (para. (d)), funding-balance elections and the regulation's worked examples were not applied; the size of the late-installment surcharge for Harlow depends on its 2025 minimum, which is not modelled.
