## Key results

- Interest on notes and bonds accrues from the dated date and is payable semiannually or quarterly on the specified interest payment dates. "If any principal or interest payment date is a Saturday, Sunday, or other day on which the Federal Reserve System is not open for business, we will make the payment (without additional interest) on the next business day."

## Limits

Treasury rule only; other markets apply their own business-day conventions, and some adjust accrual dates as well.
