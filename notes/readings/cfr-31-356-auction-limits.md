## Key results

- Sec. 356.12(b)(1): "You may not bid noncompetitively for more than $10 million", except bids made solely through a request to reinvest a maturing security held directly with Treasury.
- Sec. 356.22(a): the maximum award to any noncompetitive bidder is $10 million, with the same reinvestment exception. Sec. 356.22(b): a competitive award is capped at 35 percent of the offering amount less the bidder's reportable net long position (worked example: $3.5 billion cap on a $10 billion note, $2.5 billion with a $1 billion net long position).

## Limits

Current federal rule only; auction announcements set each auction's offering amount and timing. The October 2026 amendment (`fr-2026-21069`) did not change these sections.
