## Key results

- MCEV = adjusted net asset value + value of in-force; VIF allows for the time value of options and guarantees, frictional cost of required capital and cost of residual non-hedgeable risks; VNB is the contribution of the year's new policies, net of reinsurance, tax and minorities (p. 6).
- 2017: VNB 782 (PVFP 1,094, TVOG -167, FCRC -22, CRNHR -123); APE 3,316; PVNBP 28,201; VNB/APE 23.6%, VNB/PVNBP 2.8% (p. 12).
- Sensitivities (EUR m, MCEV 23,241): rates +50 bp +1,248, -50 bp -1,158; surrenders -10% +251; costs -10% +306; longevity claims -5% -61; mortality and disability claims -5% +177; swaption volatility +25% -734 (p. 21); definitions p. 22.
