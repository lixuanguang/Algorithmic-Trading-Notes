## Key results

- The sample CV is the ratio of the standard deviation to the mean.
- It should typically be used only for data on a ratio scale, continuous with a meaningful zero; near a zero mean it is sensitive to small changes in the mean.
- The relative standard deviation is the absolute value of the CV in percent.

## Limits

The page does not discuss negative means; the entry's statement that a CV of returns can be negative or meaningless is an interpretation from the ratio-scale requirement, illustrated numerically.
