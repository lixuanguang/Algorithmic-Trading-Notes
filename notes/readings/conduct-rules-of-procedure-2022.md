## Key results

- Staff propose a sanction; if the covered person rejects it, a Hearing Panel of Disciplinary Review Committee members decides.
- The Hearing Panel decides whether the violation occurred by a preponderance of the evidence, that is, more likely than not.
- Sanctions include suspension or revocation of membership and of the right to use the designation; summary suspension and reinstatement have their own procedures.
