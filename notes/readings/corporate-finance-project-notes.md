## Key results

- V.a: for a company analysed in US dollars, use the 10-year bond rate; in euros, the lowest 10-year euro government bond rate; emerging-market government rates contain default risk.
- V.b: survey, historical and implied premiums. A US analysis can use the historical premium; "If you have a non-US company, don't even bother estimating a historical premium". The implied premium is the internal rate of return on the index from expected dividends and buybacks minus the riskfree rate; it is a market-wide premium. "Should you use this premium or the historical premium? Depends upon your point of view. If you want your valuation to be market neutral, use the current implied equity risk premium. If you believe that numbers revert back to historical averages, use the historical premium."
- V.c: regression betas come with standard errors; consider the range, not only the point estimate; services differ in index, return period and interval.
