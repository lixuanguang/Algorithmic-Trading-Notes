## Key results

- Active Share = 1/2 sum |w_fund,i - w_index,i|; a fund is 100% index plus a zero-net active long-short portfolio (sec. 2.2).
- Tracking error weights correlated bets; Active Share weights all bets equally; together they separate diversified and concentrated stock pickers, factor bettors and closet indexers (pp. 2-3).
