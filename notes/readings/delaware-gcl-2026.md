## Key results

- 151(a): classes and series with full, limited or no voting powers and preferences, set in the certificate or by board resolution under authority in it; 151(b): redeemable stock if fully voting stock remains.
- 170(a): dividends out of surplus or, if none, out of net profits of the current or preceding year.
- 212(a): one vote per share unless the certificate provides otherwise; 212(b): proxies valid three years unless longer; 214: cumulative voting if the certificate provides; 216: default quorum a majority (not below one third), majority of shares present for other matters, plurality for directors.
