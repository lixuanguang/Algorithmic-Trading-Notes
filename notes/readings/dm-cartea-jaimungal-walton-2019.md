## Key results

- Risk-neutral brokers quote zero-expected-profit spreads that recover losses to latency arbitrageurs; last look lowers quoted spreads with one venue; with two venues an equilibrium may keep both or one, and a last-look venue may quote wider spreads (abstract). A model, not evidence.
- No central FX exchange; G10 pairs on more than 20 ECNs; banks' own platforms and APIs (p. 2).
