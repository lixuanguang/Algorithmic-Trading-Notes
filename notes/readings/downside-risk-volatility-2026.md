## Key results

- Eq. (1): semideviation with respect to a benchmark B, the square root of (1/T) times the sum of squared shortfalls min(R_t - B, 0), T the number of observations; returns above B do not contribute; B = 0 in the rest of the paper.
- Markowitz (1959) considered the semideviation and chose the standard deviation for cost and convenience (p. 1, as reported).
- Eq. (3): probability of loss as the share of negative returns.

## Limits

The paper's empirical rankings of assets by volatility and downside measures were not used. Other conventions (n - 1, or the number of shortfalls) come from practice and are stated in the entry as alternatives, not from this paper.
