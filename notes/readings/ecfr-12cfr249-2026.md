## Key results

- Applicability (249.1(b)(1)): GSIB BHCs, GSIB depository institutions, Category II and III Board-regulated institutions, Category IV with $50 billion or more in average weighted short-term wholesale funding; covered nonbank companies; Board determination.
- NSFR >= 1.0 on an ongoing basis (249.100(a)); RSF amount = adjustment percentage x sum of RSF-weighted assets, commitments and derivatives RSF (249.105(a)).
- Adjustment percentages (249.105(b), table 1): GSIB and GSIB depository institution 100; Category II 100; Category III with $75 billion or more wSTWF (and their consolidated Category III subsidiaries) 100; other Category III 85; Category IV with $50 billion or more wSTWF 70.
- Category IV (252.5(e)): $100 billion or more of average total consolidated assets (or combined US assets for an FBO), not a GSIB, not Category II or III.
