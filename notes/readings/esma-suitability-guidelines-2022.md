## Key results

- Firms collect knowledge and experience, financial situation including capacity for loss, and objectives including risk tolerance.
- Firms must check the reliability of information rather than rely on clients' self-assessment.
- Information must be kept up to date, with the frequency matched to the client.

## Limits

EU supervisory guidelines; national application varies.
