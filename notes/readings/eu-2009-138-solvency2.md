## Key results

- Assets and liabilities at transfer value, without own-credit adjustment (Art. 75).
- Technical provisions = best estimate (probability-weighted PV of future cash flows at the relevant risk-free curve) + risk margin; risk margin = cost of providing own funds equal to the SCR over the run-off (Art. 77).
- SCR = VaR of basic own funds at 99.5% over one year, covering existing business and new business over 12 months (Art. 101(3)).
- Life underwriting sub-modules: mortality, longevity, disability-morbidity, life-expense, revision, lapse, life catastrophe (Art. 105(3)); market module includes interest rate, equity, property, spread, currency, concentration (Art. 105(5)).
- Annex IV: market-life correlation 0.25.
