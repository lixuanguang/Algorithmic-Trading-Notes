## Key results

- Risk margin = CoC x sum of discounted future SCRs of a reference undertaking; CoC rate 6% (Arts. 37-39).
- Life correlations: mortality-longevity -0.25, mortality-lapse 0, longevity-lapse 0.25 (Art. 136).
- Shocks: mortality +15% (Art. 137); longevity -20% (Art. 138); disability +35%/+25%, recovery -20% (Art. 139); expenses +10% (Art. 140); lapse: largest of +50%, -50% (capped at 20 points) and mass lapse of 40% of other policies, 70% for certain group pension policies (Art. 142).
