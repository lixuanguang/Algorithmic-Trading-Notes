## Key results

- Securitisation: credit risk of an exposure or pool is tranched; payments depend on the pool; subordination determines the distribution of losses (Art. 2(1)); SSPE structure intended to isolate its obligations from the originator's (Art. 2(2)).
- Originator, sponsor or original lender retains a material net economic interest of not less than 5%: of each tranche, of the originator's interest in revolving exposures, of random exposures, or the first-loss tranche (Art. 6).
