## Key results

- 3a: companies may identify shareholders; member states may set a threshold no higher than 0.5 percent. 3b-3c: intermediaries transmit information and facilitate voting; electronic confirmation of votes. 3d: non-discriminatory charges.
- 3g: engagement policy and annual disclosure of voting, significant votes and use of proxy advisers, comply or explain. 3j: proxy advisers' code of conduct and disclosures. 9a-9b: votes on remuneration policy (binding or advisory) and report.
