## Key results

- Art. 5(2), first subparagraph, original: (a) FY from 1 Jan 2024 for large PIEs and PIE parents of large groups with more than 500 employees; (b) FY from 1 Jan 2025 for other large undertakings and parents; (c) FY from 1 Jan 2026 for listed SMEs and others.
- Art. 5(2), second subparagraph: the measures complying with Art. 1(14) (third-country reporting, Art. 40a) apply for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2028; not amended by Directive 2025/794 or 2026/470.
- As amended: point (b) moved to 1 January 2027 by Directive 2025/794 Art. 1; point (a) limited to FY 2024-2026, point (b) given the EUR 450m / 1,000-employee thresholds and point (c) deleted by Directive 2026/470 Art. 3(1), which also lets Member States exempt undertakings below those thresholds for FY 2025-2026.
