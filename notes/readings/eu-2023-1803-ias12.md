## Key results

- Para. 12: current tax unpaid is a liability; an overpayment is an asset.
- Para. 58: current and deferred tax go to profit or loss except tax arising from items recognised in OCI or equity, or from a business combination.
- Para. 61A: tax relating to items recognised outside profit or loss is recognised in OCI or directly in equity accordingly; para. 62(a): revaluation of property, plant and equipment is an OCI item.
- Paras. 79-80: major components of tax expense disclosed, including current tax and deferred tax; para. 81(a) and (ab): tax charged directly to equity and tax on each OCI component disclosed.
- Reconciliation of tax expense to accounting profit times the applicable rate, or of the average effective rate to the applicable rate (para. 81(c)).
