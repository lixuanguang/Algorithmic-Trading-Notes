## Key results

- Functional currency: 'the currency of the primary economic environment in which the entity operates' (para. 8); normally where it primarily generates and expends cash; primary indicators on prices and costs (para. 9), supporting indicators on financing and retained receipts (para. 10), foreign-operation indicators (para. 11), judgement with priority to para. 9 (para. 12).
- Transactions at the spot rate (para. 21); monetary items at the closing rate (para. 23); exchange differences in profit or loss (para. 28) except on a net investment in a foreign operation (para. 32).
- Translation: assets and liabilities at the closing rate, income and expenses at transaction rates or an approximating average, differences in OCI (paras. 39-40); hyperinflationary functional currency: IAS 29 restatement then everything at the closing rate (paras. 42-43); cumulative differences reclassified on disposal (para. 48).
- Disclosure of exchange differences in profit or loss and in OCI with a reconciliation, and of a presentation currency different from the functional currency (paras. 52-53).

## Limits

EU-adopted text without later amendments (the 2023 lack-of-exchangeability amendments were not checked).
