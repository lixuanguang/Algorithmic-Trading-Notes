## Key results

- 20% or more of voting power presumed to give significant influence, less than 20% presumed not, unless clearly demonstrated otherwise (para. 5); evidenced by board representation, policy participation, material transactions, interchange of managers, essential technical information (para. 6).
- Equity method: cost, adjusted for the investor's share of profit or loss; distributions reduce the carrying amount (para. 10); goodwill stays in the carrying amount, not amortised; share of profit adjusted for fair-value depreciation (para. 32); losses stop at the interest, including long-term interests (para. 38).
