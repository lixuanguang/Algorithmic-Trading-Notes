## Key results

- Definitions of cash, cash equivalents, operating, investing and financing activities (para. 6); classification by activity (para. 10); one transaction can be split, e.g. a loan repayment's interest and principal (para. 12).
- Operating examples and the rule that proceeds from selling plant are investing although the gain enters profit (para. 14); only expenditure that creates a recognised asset may be investing (para. 16); financing examples include lease principal (para. 17).
- Direct or indirect method (para. 18); direct method encouraged and derivable by adjusting revenue and expenses for working-capital changes and non-cash items (para. 19); indirect adjustments (para. 20).
- Exchange-rate effect on cash shown separately (para. 28); interest and dividend classification choices for non-financial entities (paras. 31-34); non-cash investing and financing excluded and disclosed (paras. 43-44).
- Before IFRS 18: interest paid operating or financing for non-financial entities (para. 33); taxes paid operating unless specifically identified with investing or financing (paras. 35-36).

## Limits

Original 2023 act, not a consolidated text: later EU amendments (Regulations 2024/1317, 2025/1331 and 2026/338) are not reflected. The supplier-finance disclosures added in 2024 were not read. Not the IASB's own publication, which requires registration.
