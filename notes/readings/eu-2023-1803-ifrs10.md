## Key results

- An investor controls an investee 'if and only if' it has power, exposure or rights to variable returns, and the ability to use its power to affect those returns (para. 7).
- Non-controlling interests within equity, separately from the parent's (para. 22); profit and OCI attributed to owners of the parent and NCI, even if NCI goes into deficit (para. B94).
