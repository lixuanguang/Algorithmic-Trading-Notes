## Key results

- A joint operation gives rights to the assets and obligations for the liabilities; a joint venture gives rights to the net assets (paras. 15-16).
- Joint operators recognise their assets, liabilities, revenue and expenses (para. 20); joint venturers use the equity method (para. 24), so proportionate consolidation is not used for joint ventures.
