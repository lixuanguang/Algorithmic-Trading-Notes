## Key results

- B7: matrix pricing is a market-approach technique valuing debt securities by their relationship to benchmark quoted securities rather than their own quotes. 79(a): an entity with many similar securities may use it as a practical expedient, but the measurement then falls in a lower level of the fair value hierarchy.
- Paras. 2, 9 and 57 (read 2026-10-20): fair value is a market-based exit price in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date, not an entity-specific measurement; the transaction (entry) price can differ from fair value.
