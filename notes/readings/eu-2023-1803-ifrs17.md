## Key results

- Initial measurement: fulfilment cash flows (estimates of future cash flows, discounting and financial risk, risk adjustment for non-financial risk) plus the contractual service margin (para. 32).
- Risk adjustment: compensation the entity requires for bearing non-financial risk (para. 37). CSM: unearned profit, set so that no gain arises on initial recognition (para. 38); end-of-period CSM is profit not yet recognised for future service (para. 43); roll-forward with new contracts, accretion, changes relating to future service, release (para. 44).
- Onerous groups: net outflow at initial recognition, or unfavourable changes exceeding the CSM; the loss goes to profit or loss and a loss component is set up (paras. 47-49).
- Insurance revenue depicts services provided; investment components and premium information inconsistent with para. 83 are excluded (paras. 83-85).
- The Regulation's recitals record the EU exemption from annual cohorts for certain intergenerationally mutualised and cash-flow-matched contracts.

## Limits

Measurement detail (B paragraphs), the variable fee approach and the premium allocation approach were not read.
