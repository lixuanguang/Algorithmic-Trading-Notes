## Key results

- Identifiable assets and liabilities recognised separately from goodwill at fair value (10, 18, B31); goodwill as a residual (32).
- NCI at fair value or at its proportionate share of identifiable net assets (para. 19); goodwill = consideration + NCI + previously held interest - net identifiable assets (para. 32).
