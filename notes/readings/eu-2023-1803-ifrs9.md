## Key results

- Classification by business model and SPPI: amortised cost, FVOCI, FVPL (4.1.1-4.1.4).
- Equity FVOCI election: irrevocable, at initial recognition, instrument by instrument; unavailable for equity held for trading and for contingent consideration of an acquirer under IFRS 3 (4.1.4, 5.7.5, B5.7.1); held for trading defined in Appendix A (near-term sale, short-term profit-taking portfolio, derivative); OCI amounts never recycled, dividends in profit or loss unless a recovery of cost (5.7.6, B5.7.1). Superseded wording (2026-10-20): the earlier entry text omitted the timing and both exclusions.
- Liabilities at amortised cost; own-credit changes on FVO liabilities in OCI (4.2.1, 5.7.7); initial measurement and effective interest (5.1.1, 5.4.1).
- Expected credit losses: 12-month or lifetime after significant increase (5.5.1-5.5.5).
- Irrevocable election to present fair-value changes of an equity instrument not held for trading in OCI; dividends in profit (paras. 5.7.5-5.7.6).
