## Key results

- Classification of Liabilities as Current or Non-current (IASB January 2020, deferred July 2020) and Non-current Liabilities with Covenants (October 2022); EU application at the latest from financial years starting on or after 1 January 2024 (Art. 2).
- Current if due within 12 months or no right at the reporting date to defer for 12 months (69); covenants tested on or before the date affect the right, later ones do not but are disclosed (72A-72B, 76ZA); breach before year-end makes the liability current despite later waiver (74); roll-over right makes it non-current (73).
