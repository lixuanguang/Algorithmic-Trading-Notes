## Key results

- Supplier finance arrangements: finance providers pay suppliers; the entity pays the provider at or after the supplier's date (44G).
- Disclose terms, carrying amounts and line items, amounts already paid to suppliers, ranges of payment due dates against comparable payables, and non-cash changes (44H); annual periods from 1 January 2024 (62; Art. 2).
