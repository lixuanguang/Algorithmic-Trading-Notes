## Key results

- Arts. 2-3: in force 10 November 2026; applies to financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with optional use for 2026.
- ESRS 1 paras. 22-28: double materiality; two-step assessment; top-down from strategy, business model, sector and value chain.
- Paras. 38-44: impact materiality (severity from scale, scope and irremediability; likelihood for potential impacts); paras. 45-49: financial materiality (effects on development, position, performance, cash flows, access to finance and cost of capital; impacts, dependencies and other sources).
