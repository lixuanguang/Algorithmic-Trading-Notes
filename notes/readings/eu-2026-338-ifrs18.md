## Key results

- EU application at the latest from the first financial year starting on or after 1 January 2027 (Art. 2); IFRS 18 applies to annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, earlier application permitted (para. C1).
- Amended IAS 7 para. 18(b) and 20: the indirect method starts from operating profit or loss and adds operating cash flows, such as income tax, whose income or expense is outside the operating category.
- Dividends paid are financing (new para. 33A). An entity without a main business of investing in assets or financing customers classifies interest paid as financing and interest and dividends received as investing (para. 34A); other entities follow the income-statement classification (paras. 34B-34D). Former paras. 33-34 deleted.
- IAS 7 as amended: dividends paid financing (33A); interest paid financing for entities without a specified main business activity (34A); others follow the profit-or-loss classification (34B-34D); taxes on income operating unless specifically identified (35).
