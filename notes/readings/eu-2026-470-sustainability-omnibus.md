## Key results

- Thresholds: net turnover above EUR 450m and an average of more than 1,000 employees, for individual undertakings (Art. 2(4), Art. 19a(1)) and for parent undertakings on a consolidated basis (Art. 2(5), Art. 29a(1)). Art. 2(1) amends Art. 1(3) (the legal-form extension), not the reporting obligation itself.
- Third-country route (Art. 2(13), Art. 40a(1)): EU subsidiary or branch with net turnover above EUR 200m, where the third-country undertaking's EU turnover exceeded EUR 450m in each of the last two financial years; derogation for some financial holding undertakings.
- Timing (Art. 3(1), amending CSRD Art. 5(2)): first wave (large PIEs above 500 employees) for FY 2024-2026 only; the new thresholds in point (b), whose start Directive 2025/794 set at FY 2027; optional Member State exemption for FY 2025-2026 below the new thresholds.
- Transposition of Arts 1-3 by 19 March 2027 and Art. 4 by 26 July 2028 (Art. 5(1)); entry into force on the twentieth day after publication (Art. 6).
- Superseded first reading (Batch L3, kept for the record): "Mandatory sustainability reporting limited to undertakings exceeding net turnover of EUR 450m and 1,000 employees on average (Art. 2(1))". It cited the wrong point and omitted groups, third-country undertakings and application years.

## Limits

A directive: application depends on national transposition, which was not checked by Member State. Directive 2025/794 is now cited for the 2027 start (eu-2025-794-stop-the-clock); the third-country start of FY 2028 comes from CSRD Art. 5(2) (eu-2022-2464-csrd).
