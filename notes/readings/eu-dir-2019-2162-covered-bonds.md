## Key results

- Covered bond: issued by a credit institution, secured by cover assets to which investors have direct recourse as preferred creditors; cover pool segregated (Art. 3(1), (3)).
- Dual recourse: claim on the issuer; on insolvency a priority claim on the cover assets; any shortfall pari passu with unsecured creditors (Art. 4(1)).
- All liabilities covered by cover assets (Art. 15); liquidity buffer covering the maximum cumulative net outflow over 180 days (Art. 16).
