## Key results

- Retail trading over 60% of U.S. option volume; nearly 90% of PFOF from three wholesalers (Citadel, Susquehanna, Wolverine) by 2021Q2 (abstract; pp. 2, 11).
- All U.S. options must execute on exchanges; wholesalers' affiliated market makers bring paired orders to price-improvement auctions; affiliated fee $0.05/contract against $0.50 for a responder, net rebate $0.30 (p. 11, fn. 15).
- SLIM retail proxy from OPRA's single-leg non-ISO price-improvement flag; multi-leg auctions excluded as noisier (p. 12).
- Table I: quoted spread = (ask - bid)/midpoint across exchanges; effective = 2|price - mid|/mid. SLIM 13.7% quoted, 6.7% effective; non-SLIM 11.6%, 8.6%. Weekly options 12.6%/6.6% (SLIM); ATM about 70% of trades, quoted 8.7%; slightly OTM 28.7% (pp. 14-17).
- Aggregate SLIM trading costs to midquote $6.4bn against about $900mn commissions; 10-day-horizon net losses $2.1bn (pp. 4-5).
