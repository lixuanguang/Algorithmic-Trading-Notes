## Data and timing

- 1 March to 31 May 2000 (p. 13). E-minis traded only on Globex; regular index futures on the CME floor in normal hours and on Globex otherwise (p. 6).

## Key results

- S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100: most price discovery in the E-minis; ETFs significant but small (abstract, p. 34).
- S&P 400: ETF and regular futures both contribute; the ETF "appears to dominate" in this draft (abstract, p. 34). The catalogue describes the published result as shared.
- Sector ETFs contribute modestly (abstract, p. 34).
- Earlier literature: futures lead cash, weaker reverse effects (p. 5).
