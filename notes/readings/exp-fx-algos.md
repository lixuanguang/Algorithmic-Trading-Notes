## Key results

- Risk transfer by RFQ or RFS at the full ticket; spread as the direct cost (p. 5); RFQ and RFM defined (glossary, p. 43).
- TCA: slippage, impact, ratio of rejected trades, benchmarks at arrival, during and after; no consolidated tape (Box B, p. 11).
- Distribution channels: voice/chat, API, single- and multi-dealer platforms (Graph 3, p. 12); agency, principal and riskless principal (p. 13).
- Fragmented market, primary venues as reference prices (p. 22); Box D: anonymous firm against disclosed non-firm (last look) pools, principal against franchise (internalised) liquidity, internalisation ratios "as high as 90%" with definitional caveats (p. 23); routing assumptions on internalised impact checked by TCA (p. 24).
- Liquidity mirage, exacerbated by last look (p. 27); internalisation reduces leakage, skewing may negate some of it (p. 27); lit against dark trade-off (p. 28).
