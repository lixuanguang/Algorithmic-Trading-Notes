## Key results

- Unwinding from July to the end of 2007; mini-unwind 1 August 10:45-11:30, sustained unwind from the open on 6 August to 1 p.m.
- Market-making losses in the week of 6 August only; reversal on 10 August; 8:1 levered book-to-market down 24% by 7 August close.
