## Key results

- Final rules align listed issuers' disclosures with UK SRS on a comply-or-explain basis across S1 and S2, including climate and Scope 3 (1.2, 1.7).
- Scope: UKLR 6 commercial companies, UKLR 14 international commercial companies (secondary listing), UKLR 15 depositary receipts, UKLR 16 non-equity and non-voting equity shares, UKLR 22 transition; not UKLR 11-13 or 17-19 (3.6-3.7).
- Application: accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, first reporting in 2028 (1.11, 3.12); relief of one year for Scope 3 and two years for non-climate S1 (3.13-3.14); earlier periods may stay on the TCFD-aligned rules or adopt early (3.19).
- Instrument FCA 2026/56 made 24 September 2026, in force 1 January 2027 (Appendix 1, para. C).
