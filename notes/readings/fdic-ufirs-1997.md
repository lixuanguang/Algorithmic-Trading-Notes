## Key results

- Six components: capital adequacy, asset quality, management, earnings, liquidity, sensitivity to market risk; ratings 1 (strongest) to 5 (weakest) (p. 752).
- 'The composite rating is not derived by computing an arithmetic average of the component ratings'; components may be weighted differently (p. 752).
- Sensitivity to market risk: the degree to which changes in interest rates, FX, commodity or equity prices can adversely affect earnings or economic capital (p. 756).
