## Key results

- Agency CMBS loans carry call protection - lockouts, yield maintenance, point penalties, defeasance - but prepayments remain hard to predict, e.g. floating-rate refinancing, cash-out refinancing, penalties that decline with seasoning; involuntary prepayments (defaults) in downturns (pp. 1-2).

## Limits

Supervisory guidance for Federal Home Loan Banks; not a description of non-agency CMBS structures.
