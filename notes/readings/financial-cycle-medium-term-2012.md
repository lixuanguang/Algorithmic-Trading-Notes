## Key results

- The financial cycle is identified with the medium-term (8-30 year) co-movement of credit, the credit-to-GDP ratio and property prices; equity prices are more volatile at short-term frequencies and co-move less (pp. 1-2, 19).
- Seven countries (Australia, Germany, Japan, Norway, Sweden, the United Kingdom, the United States), 1960-2011 (pp. 2-3).
- Average duration about 16 years (64 quarters); nearly 20 years for cycles peaking after 1998, 11 before (p. 2; Table 8, pp. 16-17).
- All systemic banking crises of domestic origin occur at or close to financial-cycle peaks; three post-1985 peaks are not close to a crisis, each with serious bank strain (p. 2; p. 17).
- Table 9 (p. 20): average peak-to-trough real GDP change in recessions of -2.2% during financial-cycle expansions and -3.4% during contractions (full sample, 17 and 21 observations); the authors call the results indicative because the sample is small.
