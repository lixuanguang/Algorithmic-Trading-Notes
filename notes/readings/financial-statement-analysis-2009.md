- Equity method as 'one-line consolidation'; consolidation does not affect total equity or net income (p. 273); hidden assets and liabilities of investees (pp. 274-275).
- BritCo translation adjustment 85,000 = 350,000 x 0.20 + 150,000 x 0.10 (pp. 304-306); exposure = net monetary position (temporal) or net investment (current rate) (pp. 307-308); translated income varies directly with rates (p. 310).
