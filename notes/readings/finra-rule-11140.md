## Key results

- (b)(1): for cash dividends under 25 percent of value, the ex-dividend date is the record date (if a business day). (b)(2): for distributions of 25 percent or more, including splits, the ex-date is the first business day after the payable date.

## Limits

US over-the-counter and exchange practice; other markets follow their own settlement cycles.
