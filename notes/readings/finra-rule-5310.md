## Key results

- Reasonable diligence to find the best market so that the price is as favourable as possible; factors: character of the market, size and type, markets checked, accessibility of the quotation, order terms ((a)(1)).
- Routing or internalising firms must review execution quality regularly and rigorously, at least quarterly, by security and order type, comparing markets on price improvement, fill likelihood, speed, size, costs, customer needs and payment for order flow (.09).
