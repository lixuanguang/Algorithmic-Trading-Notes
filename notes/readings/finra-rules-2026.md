## Key results

- 4210(b): initial margin at least the greater of Regulation T, the maintenance amount, FINRA's specific requirements and $2,000 of equity.
- 4210(c): 25 percent of market value long; short stock below $5: greater of $2.50 a share or 100 percent; at $5 or above: greater of $5 a share or 30 percent.
- 4210(d)(1): members set house requirements.
