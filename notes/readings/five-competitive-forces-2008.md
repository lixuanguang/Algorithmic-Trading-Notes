## Key results

- The five forces define industry structure and its medium- and long-run profitability (pp. 2-3, 10).
- Entry threat caps prices or forces investment (p. 3); suppliers capture value where cost increases cannot be passed on (p. 6); buyers force prices down or service up (p. 7); substitutes cap prices (p. 8); conditions for price rivalry (p. 9); growth is not attractiveness, government not a sixth force (p. 10).
- Pp. 12-15 (read 2026-10-20): positioning where forces are weakest (Paccar focused on owner-operators, who paid about a 10 percent premium); exploiting and shaping industry change; defining the relevant industry by product and geographic scope; typical steps in industry analysis (p. 15).
