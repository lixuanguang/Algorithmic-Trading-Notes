## Key results

- Effective 15 October 2026; the changes to secs. 356.14 and 356.23 apply from the first auction on Treasury's upgraded auction system. Submitters must give each noncompetitive customer's name and bid amount before the noncompetitive close; summary bid amounts end.
- Terminology aligned to "high yield, high discount rate, or high discount margin"; TIPS dated-date and index-ratio wording clarified without changing calculations. Secs. 356.12(b)(1) and 356.22 are not amended, so the $10 million limits stand.
