## Key results

- Pairs are matched by minimum distance between normalised price series; positions open when prices diverge by two historical standard deviations.
- Top-pairs portfolios earned about 12% annualised excess return in 1962-1997, reduced but still positive after bid-ask bounce and costs (sec. I).
