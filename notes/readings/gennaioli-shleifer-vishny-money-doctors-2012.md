## Key results

- Investors delegate to managers they trust, much as patients trust doctors; trust lowers investors' perceived risk.
- Managers compete by setting fees, but because of trust fees do not fall to costs.
- In the model managers underperform the market net of fees, yet investors still prefer to delegate.

## Limits

Model, not an empirical test; used only for the role of trust.
