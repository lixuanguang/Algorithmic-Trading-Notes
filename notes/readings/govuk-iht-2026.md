## Key results

- 40 percent above the GBP 325,000 nil-rate band; 36 percent if 10 percent of the net estate goes to charity.
- Residence nil-rate band of GBP 175,000 for a home passing to direct descendants, tapered above GBP 2 million.
- Gifts exempt if the donor survives seven years; taper relief on tax for gifts within seven years; annual, small-gift and regular-income exemptions.

- Final audit correction pass (2026-10-20): the gifts page's taper table (3-4 years 32, 4-5 years 24, 5-6 years 16, 6-7 years 8 percent) and annual exemption (3,000, carried forward one year) rechecked. The residence band taper and its interaction with lifetime gifts are taken from `govuk-rnrb-guidance-2019` and `hmrc-ihtm46023-2026`, not from these overview pages.
