## Key results

- Annual allowance GBP 60,000, tapered for adjusted income above GBP 260,000.
- Relief at the marginal rate on contributions within the allowance.
- Tax-free lump sums usually 25 percent, capped by a lump sum allowance of GBP 268,275.
