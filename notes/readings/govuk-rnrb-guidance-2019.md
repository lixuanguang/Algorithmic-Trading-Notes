## Key results

- The band is reduced by £1 for every £2 by which the estate exceeds the £2 million taper threshold; estate value is assets less debts, before exemptions and reliefs.
- It is capped at the value of the home passing to direct descendants; it does not apply to lifetime gifts, which can use up the basic threshold while the band stays available to the estate.
- Unused band can transfer from a late spouse as a percentage; taper can reduce the transferable amount. Worked example: £2.1m estate cuts a £125,000 band by £50,000.
