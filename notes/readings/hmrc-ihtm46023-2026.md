## Key results

- The taper threshold applies to the value of the estate after liabilities but before exemptions or reliefs ("E", IHTA 1984 s. 8D(d)); the worked example excludes lifetime gifts from E.
- Threshold £2,000,000 for 2017-18 to 2027-28 (s. 8D(6)-(8)); later policy papers keep it frozen.
