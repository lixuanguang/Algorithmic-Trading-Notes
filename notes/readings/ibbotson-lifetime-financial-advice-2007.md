## Key results

- Human capital, the present value of future earnings, is usually the largest asset of a young investor and declines with age while financial capital grows.
- Its risk and correlation with markets should shape the financial portfolio: bond-like earnings support more equities.
- Life insurance hedges human capital against mortality; annuities hedge longevity in retirement.
