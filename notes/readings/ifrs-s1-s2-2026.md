## Key results

- S1 para. 3: risks and opportunities that could reasonably be expected to affect cash flows, access to finance or cost of capital; para. 18: materiality judged by primary users of general purpose financial reports.
- S1 E1 and S2 C1: annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2024, earlier application permitted with the other standard; S1 E5: first-year climate-only relief.
- S2 C1B: Amendments to Greenhouse Gas Emissions Disclosures (December 2025) apply from 1 January 2027.
