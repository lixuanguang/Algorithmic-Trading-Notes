## Key results

- Geoeconomic fragmentation: "a policy-driven reversal of global economic integration"; channels are trade, migration, capital flows, technology diffusion and global public goods.
- Long-term output losses from trade fragmentation from 0.2% to 7% of world GDP, 8-12% in some countries with technological decoupling; EMs and low-income countries most at risk (p. 4).
- Export bans were about 90% of trade restrictions at the height of the pandemic; national-security restrictions surged in 2020 (p. 10; the earlier note's p. 9 was a locator error, since both copies put this passage on PDF p. 12).
- Box Figure 1.1 (p. 15) collects long-term trade-fragmentation losses from several studies; the note warns that they are not directly comparable (some are global, others regional) and are not an upper bound, because other channels are omitted.
