## Key results

- Eq. (1): nominal yield = real yield + expected inflation + inflation risk premium; eq. (2): real yield = expected average future real short rate + real term premium; the premia compensate for inflation risk and real-rate risk.
- Eq. (3): TIPS yield = real yield + TIPS liquidity premium; eq. (4): TIPS inflation compensation = expected inflation + inflation risk premium - TIPS liquidity premium.
- The DKW (2018) model is an affine no-arbitrage term-structure model, first estimated over 1990-2013; the note extends it to 1983 onward. The liquidity premium was high at TIPS launch and in 2008-2009.

## Limits

Model-based estimates for U.S. Treasuries; not observed premia, and the decomposition depends on the model specification. The published JFQA article was not read.
