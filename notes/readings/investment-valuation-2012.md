## Key results

- Bias in valuation and equity research; remedies (pp. 2-3); analysts' forecast accuracy fades with horizon (pp. 282-284).
- Stable growth constraints, nominal/real consistency, length of high growth, stable-firm characteristics, reinvestment = g/ROC (pp. 307-313).
- Options outstanding valued with an option-pricing model at the estimated value per share (iterate), deducted from equity, then divided by primary shares; treasury stock ignores time value and overstates value per share; future grants as an expense, with current margins locking in the current grant rate (pp. 445-448).
- FCFE and FCFF definitions and reconciliation (pp. 364, 380-381); sector-specific multiples, uses and limits (pp. 571-576).
- Pp. 1-25 (2026-10-20): price against value; myths of valuation; roles of valuation; intrinsic value defined (p. 12); equity against firm valuation and mismatches (pp. 12-15); DCF, relative, asset-based and contingent-claim approaches.
- Pp. 285-289, 323-348: fundamental growth (b x ROE); the DDM, Gordon model and its limits, implied growth, two-stage, H and three-stage models, value of growth, tests of DDMs.
- Pp. 453-517, 542-545: relative-valuation steps, distributions, fundamentals, comparables and controls; P/E (definitions, fundamentals, PEG, normalised earnings), EV/EBITDA and its determinants, P/B and P/S and their justified forms; pp. 75, 124-132: earnings and price momentum.
- Pp. 596-597: excess-return (residual income) model for equity. Pp. 702-728: acquisition types, evidence, motives, synergy valuation, biases and EPS accretion.

- Private firms: total beta = market beta / correlation for an undiversified owner (pp. 672-673); restricted-stock discounts about 34-35%; Silber regression (pp. 685-686); motive changes the inputs (Table 24.2, pp. 688-689).
- EVA = (ROC - WACC) x capital invested; MVA = PV of expected EVA (pp. 870-874).
- Scenario analysis has four components; best/worst cases may be infeasible; 'garbage in, garbage out' (pp. 894-897); simulation steps: probabilistic variables, distributions (historical, cross-sectional, statistical), correlation check, run (pp. 908-910); simulations give a full distribution (p. 919).
