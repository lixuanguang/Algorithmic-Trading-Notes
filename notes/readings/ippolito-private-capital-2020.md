## Key results

- Fees 1.0% (debt) to 2.0% (equity) on commitments, stepping down; hurdle 7-8% (PE), 3-4% (PD); catch-up clause: carry holders take 100% until they have 25% of the investors' preferred return, then 80/20 (pp. 15-16).
- DPI, TVPI definitions; J-curve; target returns by instrument (Table 1.3) and stage (Table 1.4) (pp. 16-21).
- Unitranche: single agreement, agreement among lenders, bullet 5-7 years, make-whole then 101, up to 5.5x (pp. 43-45); mezzanine: contractual subordination, cash plus PIK, warrants, standstill (pp. 39-43).
- Growth capital method R = I(1+IRR)^t, S = R/V, y = x S/(1-S) (pp. 173-175).
- Typical LBO structure: senior 50-60% (3.5-4.0x), subordinated 25-35% (total 4.5-5.5x), equity 20-25% (pp. 192-193); sponsors target 25-35% IRR over 3-4 years (p. 198).
- Covenant ratio guidance: senior ICR above 2x (1.5x acceptable), DSCR at least 1.5x for less stable cash flow, leverage up to 4x (pp. 236-237).
- Exit routes: IPO (lock-up), trade sale (synergy premium), secondary buyout, buybacks, options, recaps (pp. 220-225).

## Limits

Discrepancy: the dilution adjustment divides by (1 + D1)(1 + D2) while describing D as the share taken by later investors; that is correct only if D is new shares relative to existing shares (p. 175). Practitioner ranges are European and US market practice before 2020.
