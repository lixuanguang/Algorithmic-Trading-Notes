## Key results

- A foreigner is resident after at least 183 days in Singapore in the previous calendar year.
- A foreigner who stays or works in Singapore continuously for three consecutive years is resident for all three under an administrative concession; a stay straddling two calendar years totalling 183 days also counts.
- Residents are taxed at progressive resident rates and may claim reliefs.
