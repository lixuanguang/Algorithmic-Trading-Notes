## Key results

- ARPA 2021 sec. 9705 added IRC 430(c)(8): shortfall amortization bases for plan years before the first plan year to which it applies are reduced to zero, and new bases are amortized over 15 plan years instead of 7 (sec. II.B).
- Applies to plan years beginning after 31 December 2021; the sponsor may elect plan years beginning after 31 December 2018, 2019 or 2020 (secs. II.B, IV.A, IV.D).
- ARPA sec. 9706 narrowed the segment-rate corridor to 95-105 percent and set a 5 percent floor on the 25-year average (sec. II.C); the current statute extends the 95-105 corridor through 2030 (IRC 430(h)(2)(C)(iv), checked separately).
- PR #33 correction pass (2026-10-21; PDF saved as `reading_materials/completion-20261020/institutional/irs-n-2021-48.pdf`): sec. III.B.1 (p. 5) lists among the items computed at the applicable segment rates "the calculation of the present value of remaining shortfall and waiver amortization installments for purposes of determining any shortfall amortization base established in the current plan year under § 430(c)(3)": a new base is net of existing installments.

## Limits

Single-employer plans only; multiemployer plans follow IRC 431. Guidance on elections; the statutory text is IRC 430 itself.
