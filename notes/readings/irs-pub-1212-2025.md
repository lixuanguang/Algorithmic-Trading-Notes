## Key results

- The rules for including OID in income as it accrues generally do not apply to tax-exempt obligations; "the de minimis rules generally do not apply to tax-exempt obligations".
- To determine gain or loss on a tax-exempt bond, basis is cost plus "the OID you would have included in income if the bond had been taxable".
- OID on debt issued after 1984 accrues by a constant yield to maturity over accrual periods; the entry's example accrues at the issue yield period by period.

## Limits

The publication lists taxable OID instruments and their accrual; the tax-exempt rules are in its exceptions and in Pub. 550. Stripped tax-exempt bonds acquired after 10 June 1987 can have a taxable part of OID, which the entry does not cover.
