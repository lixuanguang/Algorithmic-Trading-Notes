## Key results

- 2026 rate brackets: 37 percent above USD 640,600 single and 768,700 joint; 20 percent long-term gains above 545,500 single and 613,700 joint.
- Gift annual exclusion USD 19,000 per donee; basic exclusion USD 15 million; annual exclusion for gifts to a non-citizen spouse USD 194,000.
- Expatriation thresholds for covered expatriates (secs. 4.37-4.38).

- Final audit correction pass (2026-10-20): sec. 4.01 Table 1 (p. 10), married filing jointly: 35 percent over 512,450 to 768,700, 37 percent above; used for the Okafors' marginal bracket.
- PR #33 correction pass (2026-10-21): sec. 4.03 (p. 13), maximum 15 percent rate amount for joint returns 613,700 (zero rate 98,900), which lies inside the 35 percent band, so the band alone does not set the rate on qualified dividends and gains; sec. 4.14, joint standard deduction 32,200. Used, with IRC sec. 1(h), to stack the Okafors' 66,000 of dividends and gains on 571,200 of ordinary taxable income (42,500 at 15 percent, 23,500 at 20 percent).
