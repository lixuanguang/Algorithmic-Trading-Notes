## Key results

- Facts: fixed cash received; delivery in three years of 100 shares below $20, shares worth $2,000 between $20 and $25, 80 shares above $25; cash may be delivered instead; 100 shares pledged to an unrelated trustee; votes and dividends retained; no economic compulsion (p. 363).
- Holding: neither a current sale under sec. 1001 nor a constructive sale under sec. 1259; the 80-100 share variation is significant, so the contract is not a forward contract under sec. 1259(d)(1), citing S. Rep. No. 33, 105th Cong. (p. 365).
- A different outcome may be warranted under legal restraint or economic compulsion to deliver the pledged shares (pp. 364-365).
