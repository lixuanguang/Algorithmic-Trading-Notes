## Key results

- Cost approach: a buyer pays no more than the cost of an asset of equal utility, by purchase or construction, unless undue time, inconvenience or risk intervene (70.1); primary only where an asset could be recreated quickly without legal hurdles, earns no income, or the basis of value is replacement cost (70.2); otherwise corroborate (70.3).
- Replacement cost is of a modern equivalent asset, adjusted for physical deterioration and all relevant obsolescence: depreciated replacement cost (80.2-80.5).
- Physical, functional (excess capital or operating cost) and economic or external obsolescence (demand, oversupply, supply disruption, an occupier unable to pay market rent and earn a market return); for real estate external obsolescence affects land and improvements (90.2-90.7).
