## Key results

- Taxonomy: equity hedged, relative value, event-driven, opportunistic (global macro, multi-strategy), managed futures; emerging markets not a separate category; 'arbitrage' is a misnomer (pp. 12-14).
- Funds of funds: 1% and 10% above a hurdle; 30% or more of hedge fund assets in 2006 (p. 15); asymmetric performance fees cost 0.4-0.8% a year; leverage at Libor + 60-80 bp against cash at Libor - 20 bp (p. 4).
- Strategy mechanics: long/short equity long-biased (p. 37), market neutral (p. 41), short selling collateral 30-50% (p. 44), relative value short a put on market health (p. 45), convertible arbitrage gamma and carry (p. 51), volatility arbitrage (p. 58), capital structure arbitrage (p. 60), merger arbitrage (p. 64), distressed (p. 67), global macro (p. 70), managed futures trend following (pp. 75-79).
- Factor models: Fung and Hsieh extended Sharpe's style model; simple six-factor regressions give alphas of 5-12% a year; adding strategy-specific factors and AR(1) roughly halves the average alpha, half of the drop due to AR(1) (pp. 161-165).
- Mean-variance frontiers with hedge fund indices overstate the benefit; use expected shortfall or Cornish-Fisher VaR (pp. 107-109).
