## Key results

- Over 200 recessions in 14 advanced countries, 1870-2008: financial-crisis recessions cost more output; more credit-intensive expansions are followed by deeper recessions and slower recoveries in both normal and crisis recessions (abstract).
- Excess credit: the rate of change of bank loans to the non-financial sector relative to GDP over the expansion, against its mean; after five years the financial-crisis path of real GDP per capita is about 4% below the normal path (pp. 4-5).
