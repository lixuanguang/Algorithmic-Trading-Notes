## Key results

- Project finance: non-recourse debt to an SPV repaid only from project cash flow; development, construction and operating phases (pp. 97-99).
- PPP availability payments for performance irrespective of demand, reduced for poor availability (pp. 115-116).
- Greenfield, brownfield, yellowfield; funds with 10-15-year lives; listed infrastructure companies need higher IRRs (pp. 121-122).
- DSCR = NOCF/(P + I); target, lock-up and default levels; higher for merchant risk (p. 159); LLCR (p. 160); debt capped by DSCR or gearing (pp. 169-170).
- Tranches paid in order of seniority; senior typically 50-70% of the SPV; equity tranche residual (pp. 256-257). OC tests on 5-20% overcollateralisation and IC tests divert cash to senior notes when breached (p. 309).

- Project loans are drawn in stages as construction milestones are met, subject to conditions precedent (pp. 157-158); equity may be drawn first, pro rata with debt, or at the end through sponsor-guaranteed bridge loans, which raises the sponsors' IRR (p. 171).
- Table 10.1: a $700m securitisation of corporate loans and bonds in six tranches, Class A 68.5%, floating coupons over three-month Libor, unrated subordinated notes 8% (p. 260).
