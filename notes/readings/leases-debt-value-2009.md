## Key results

- Treating leases as financing: the change in the present value of lease commitments plus depreciation on the leased asset is capital expenditure (pp. 25-26).
- FCFE is unaffected: the extra capital expenditure is exactly offset by the extra net debt issued (pp. 26-27, Table 17).
- Debt netted from firm value must include the lease debt; equity value should not change for a pure reclassification, though in practice inputs such as betas can move (pp. 30-32).

## Limits

Written for US operating leases before IFRS 16 and Topic 842 put lease liabilities on balance sheets; its point about reinvestment applies to right-of-use additions under IFRS 16 when leases are treated as debt. Market data and examples date from 2008.
