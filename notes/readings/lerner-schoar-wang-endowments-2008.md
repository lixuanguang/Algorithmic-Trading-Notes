## Key results

- Sample of about 1,300 educational endowments, 1992 to 2005: the largest endowments, led by the Ivy League, pull away from the average (the rich get richer).
- Growth is driven largely by high investment returns.
- Returns are related to the quality of the student body and the use of alternative assets.
