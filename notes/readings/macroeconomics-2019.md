## Key results

- Real rate r = i - pi and Fisher equation i = r + pi (additive form).
- Ex ante real rate i - E pi, expected when the loan is made; ex post real rate i - pi, actually realised; they differ when inflation differs from expectation.
- The nominal rate "can adjust only to expected inflation", so the Fisher effect is i = r + E pi.
