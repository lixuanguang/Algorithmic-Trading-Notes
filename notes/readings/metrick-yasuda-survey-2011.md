## Key results

- PE funds: intermediaries, private companies not marked to market, monitoring and control, need to exit (pp. 5-7). Hedge-fund carry is charged on market value above cost subject to high-water marks; PE carry only on realisations (p. 7).
- Fund contracts: finite life, fees on committed capital, 20% carry, negative covenants; VC uses convertibles and staging; buyouts control and leverage (p. 33). About two-thirds of expected GP revenue is fixed (p. 35, summarising Metrick and Yasuda 2010).
