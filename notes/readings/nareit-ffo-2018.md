## Key results

- FFO = net income excluding real-estate depreciation and amortisation, gains and losses on sales of certain real estate, gains and losses from change in control, and certain impairments of depreciable real estate (p. 2).
- SEC staff accept Nareit FFO, including per share; adjusted measures must comply with Item 10(e) and may not be presented per share depending on their nature (pp. 3, 7).
- No consensus on AFFO, CAD or FAD; reconcile any adjusted measure to FFO and GAAP; disclose capital spending by category (p. 7).
