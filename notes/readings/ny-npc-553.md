## Key results

- 553(a): appropriation must be in good faith with ordinary prudence, considering listed factors, with a contemporaneous record of the board's consideration.
- 553(d): for gift instruments executed on or after the article's effective date, appropriating more than 7 percent of fair market value, determined at least quarterly and averaged over at least the five preceding years (or the fund's life if shorter), creates a rebuttable presumption of imprudence; not if the gift instrument or other law permits; spending at or below 7 percent creates no presumption of prudence.
- 553(e): older gift instruments need 90 days' notice to an available donor before 553(a) is first applied, with exceptions.

## Limits

New York only; other states' enactments of the uniform act differ (some have no presumption, some a different threshold or averaging period). The effective date of the article was not taken from this page.
