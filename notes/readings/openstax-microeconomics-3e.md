## Key results

- Breakeven where MC crosses AC; shutdown point where MC crosses AVC; between them the firm operates at a loss (8.2).
- Kinked demand: rivals match cuts but not increases, so none has a strong incentive to change price; collusion and the prisoner's dilemma (10.2).
- Four-firm ratio and HHI with examples (windshield repair; 20%x5 = 2,000 against 77% + 23x1% = 5,952 with the same CR4); limits: market definition, global competition, similar competitive conditions (11.1).
