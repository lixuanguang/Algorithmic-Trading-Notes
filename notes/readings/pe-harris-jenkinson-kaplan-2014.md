## Key results

- Burgiss data on nearly 1,400 US funds: average buyout funds beat the S&P 500 by 20-27% over a fund's life and more than 3% a year; VC outperformed in the 1990s and underperformed in the 2000s; Venture Economics data had problems.

- Distributions include the estimated value of unrealised investments at the last reporting date; the PME discounts distributions and residual value at the public-market total return over discounted contributions (p. 10).
- The Long-Nickels excess return is the fund IRR less the IRR of an index investment with the same contributions (p. 11); residual-value assumptions matter more for recent vintages (pp. 11-12).
