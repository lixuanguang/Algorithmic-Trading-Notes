## Key results

- Large heterogeneity of returns; average LBO fund returns net of fees below the S&P 500, VC below equal-weighted and above capital-weighted; persistence across a GP's funds (pp. 1-2).
- PME: discounted distributions over discounted calls at the S&P 500's realised return; PME above one beats the index net of fees; overstates risk-adjusted performance if beta exceeds one (pp. 8-9).

- Main sample: funds officially liquidated or with returns unchanged for six quarters and unrealised value under 10% of commitments; performance and cash flows reported net of management fees and carried interest (p. 6).
