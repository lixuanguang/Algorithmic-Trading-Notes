## Key results

- Indirect method steps: begin with net income; add back depreciation and other non-cash expenses; subtract gains and add losses on long-term asset disposals; subtract increases and add decreases in current assets; add increases and subtract decreases in current liabilities (sec. 16.3).
- Investing cash flows relate to long-term assets, financing cash flows to long-term liabilities and equity; net cash flow plus opening cash equals closing cash; non-cash investing and financing items disclosed separately (secs. 16.3-16.4).
- Direct method: collections from customers as opening receivables plus sales less closing receivables; payments for inventory from purchases and payables; operating expenses adjusted for prepaid and accrued balances (sec. 16.6).
- Reread 2026-10-18 (cash-flow slice): cash paid for insurance = closing prepaid + expense - opening prepaid (2,500 + 12,000 - 1,800 = 12,700); cash paid for salaries = opening payable + expense - closing payable (1,500 + 42,600 - 1,900 = 42,400); items unrelated to current operating accounts need no adjustment (sec. 16.6).
