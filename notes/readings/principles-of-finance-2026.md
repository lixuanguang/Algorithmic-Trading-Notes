## Key results

- Sec. 3.4: risk-free rate as the government's short-term borrowing rate; real rate = nominal - inflation (6 - 1 = 5 percent); lenders charge higher-risk borrowers a premium. No liquidity or maturity premium.
- Sec. 7.4: Fisher effect (1 + i) = (1 + R)(1 + h); 6 percent real and 2 percent expected inflation give 8.12 percent. The additive form is not called an approximation in the text.
- Sec. 10.2: bond price = PV of coupons + PV of par (2.25 percent, 15 years, 1.24 percent yield gives about 1,137.47); semiannual coupons halve the coupon and the rate and double the periods; yield found by calculator or spreadsheet; premium, discount and par bonds.
- Sec. 11.2: zero-growth value D/r (2.00 at 6 percent is 33.33); constant growth D0(1 + g)/(r - g) with r > g (5.00, 4 and 8 percent give 130.00); implied growth solved from a price (250, 10.00 and 12 percent give 7.69 percent).
- Sec. 15.1: total dollar return = dividend income + capital gain; total percent return = dividend yield + capital gain yield (TGT example, 39.20 percent); EAR = (1 + HPR)^m - 1 (14.62 percent a quarter gives 72.60 percent); arithmetic and geometric averages (CVS 2011-2020: 11.11 and 9.29 percent); the geometric is smaller unless all returns are equal; arithmetic for a typical year, geometric for compound growth.
- Sec. 2.1 (2026-10-20): sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, S corporations (at most 100 shareholders), LLCs and LLPs compared on liability, taxes, transferability and capital; over 80 percent of US business by sales conducted by corporations.
- Secs. 19.1-19.5 (2026-10-20): permanent and temporary working capital; cash conversion cycle (36.5 + 54.7 - 34 = 57.2 days); cost of forgoing 2/10 net 30 is 36.73 percent on a 360-day year; credit terms follow industry practice; ageing schedules; JIT and EOQ.
