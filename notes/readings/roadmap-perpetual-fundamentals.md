## Key results

- Perpetuals offer leveraged exposure without rollover; unlike fixed-maturity futures they are not guaranteed to converge to spot; longs pay shorts a funding rate proportional to the gap (abstract).
- No-arbitrage prices in frictionless markets and bounds with trading costs; empirical deviations larger than in traditional currency markets, co-moving across currencies, diminishing over time; an implied arbitrage strategy with high Sharpe ratios (abstract).
