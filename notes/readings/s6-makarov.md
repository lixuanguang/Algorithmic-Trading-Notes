## Key results

- Tick data, 34 exchanges in 19 countries (Kaiko); large, recurrent cross-exchange arbitrage opportunities relative to fiat, persisting hours to weeks (abstract, p. 1).
- Crypto market lacks provisions ensuring the best price, unlike the U.S. NBBO (p. 1, fn. 1).
- Dec 2017-Feb 2018: US-Korea daily average price ratio > 15%, up to 40%; Japan-US ~10%; US-Europe ~3%; within-country typically < 1% on average; at least $2 billion potential profit (pp. 1-2, fn. 2).
- Asymmetric premia outside US/Europe; co-movement; open in rapid appreciation; "Bitcoin beta" (p. 2).
- Capital controls: spread correlation rises with pairwise capital-control tightness; crypto-crypto spreads an order of magnitude smaller (p. 3).
