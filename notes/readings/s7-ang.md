## Key results

- Sovereign wealth funds, pension types and pillars, foundation 5 percent payout, individuals (chap. 1).
- SRI by exclusion shrinks diversification; costs measured against the full set (pp. 110-112).
- Registered fund types, NAV redemption, closed-end and ETF trading, disclosure, 300 percent coverage, fulcrum fees (pp. 523-527); 2/20, high-water mark and hurdle with the NAV path 100, 120, 110, 145 (pp. 581-582); separate accounts transparent (p. 502); younger managers hug benchmarks (p. 503).
- Reported illiquid returns are not returns: survivorship, infrequent trading, selection; Geltner-Ross-Zisler unsmoothing r = (r* - phi r*_-1)/(1 - phi) (pp. 416-421); the conventional illiquidity-premium view is flawed and there is no investable index (pp. 427-428).
- Hedge funds: 2% of NAV, 20% incentive over a benchmark, high-water mark example 100/120/110/145, hurdle (pp. 581-582); the incentive fee is a call option worth roughly 3-4% of assets; 2/20 equivalent to a 6.4% flat fee, 5.8% with a high-water mark, 5.3% with a 4% hurdle (pp. 584-585).
- Private equity: Table 18.1 public against private; types; pro-cyclical commitments (pp. 594-596); IRR manipulation and pooling; multiples ignore time and risk; PME (pp. 598-602); management fee on committed capital, 20% carry above an 8% hurdle, catch-up, clawback, waterfalls, portfolio company fees, side letters (pp. 610-612).
- Real estate: NCREIF quarterly autocorrelation 78% and volatility 2.2% against REITs and equities 8-9%; REIT-equity correlation 63%, NCREIF-REIT 15% (1978-2011); smoothing and selection biases; long-horizon correlation about 0.43; public leads private; REIT legal constraints; real estate a partial inflation hedge at long horizons (pp. 376-381).
- GSCI 1986-2011: 8.9% mean, 20.7% volatility, raw Sharpe 0.43; correlations with stocks 14%, bonds -9%, inflation 29% (energy 23-26%, agriculture -4%, gold 1%) (pp. 366-367).
- GSCI 1970-2011 total 11.3% = spot 6.4 + cash 5.6 + roll -0.7; 2000s roll -7.5, spot 13.0; variance almost all spot (pp. 368-369). The 1970s row adds to 21.7 against 21.6 printed; the text attributes the 2000s negative roll to backwardated oil in 2008, contradicting its own rule that backwardation gives positive roll (pp. 369-370).
- Hedge funds (alternatives batch, hedge fund entries): 40-Act exemption and features (pp. 559-562); backfill 14.7% against 7.3% printed as a 7.3% bias although the difference is 7.4 (p. 565); partial correlations of the HFR index 0.67 with equities and 0.26 with volatility, long/short equity 0.11 (pp. 572-573); Fung et al. alpha 6 bp with equity, bond and volatility factors (p. 576); lock-ups, notice periods, gates; lock-up funds earn 4-7% more (pp. 585-587).
- Alternatives allocation (alternatives batch): 'factors matter, not assets'; assets are bundles of factors; asset class labels misled investors in 2008 (pp. 195, 211). Illiquidity hurdles 0.70% (six months), about 1% (one year), 6% (ten years); optimal illiquid holdings modest (p. 438); the Swensen case rests on skill (p. 439); Harvard's 2008 liquidity crunch and $2.5bn bond issue (pp. 441-442).
