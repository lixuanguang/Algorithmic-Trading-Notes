## Key results

- A sound legal framework (GAPP 1) and a clearly defined, publicly disclosed policy purpose (GAPP 2); a sound governance framework dividing roles (GAPP 6).
- Clear, disclosed policies on funding, withdrawal and spending (GAPP 4); an investment policy consistent with objectives, risk tolerance and strategy (GAPP 18).
- Investment decisions on economic and financial grounds (GAPP 19); a framework that identifies, assesses and manages risks (GAPP 22).

## Limits

Voluntary principles; compliance is self-assessed.
