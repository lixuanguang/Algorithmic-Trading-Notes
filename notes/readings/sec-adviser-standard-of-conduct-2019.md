## Key results

- An adviser's fiduciary duty comprises a duty of care and a duty of loyalty, applying to the whole relationship.
- Loyalty requires full and fair disclosure of material facts and conflicts so that a client can give informed consent; some conflicts cannot be cured by disclosure.
- The duty cannot be waived, though its application can be shaped by agreement.
