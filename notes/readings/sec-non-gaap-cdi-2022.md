## Key results

- 102.03: the Item 10(e) two-year condition is based on the description of a charge as non-recurring, infrequent or unusual; a registrant that cannot so describe a charge may still adjust for it, subject to Regulation G and Item 10(e).
- 100.01: an adjustment not expressly prohibited can still make a measure misleading; excluding normal, recurring, cash operating expenses necessary to operate the business is an example; an expense occurring repeatedly or occasionally, including at irregular intervals, is recurring; the staff weighs the adjustment against operations, revenue-generating activities, strategy, industry and regulation.
