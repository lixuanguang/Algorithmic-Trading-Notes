## Key results

- Premium or discount: market price at NAV time less NAV, over NAV (para. (a)(1)).
- Median bid-ask spread over 30 days from NBBO at the end of each 10-second interval, rounded to a hundredth of a percent (para. (c)(1)(v)).
- Statement required if the premium or discount exceeds 2% for more than seven consecutive trading days (para. (c)(1)(vi)); custom baskets need written parameters (para. (c)(3)).
