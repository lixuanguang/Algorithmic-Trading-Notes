# Tesla 2.00% Convertible Senior Notes due 2024: prospectus supplement

Read for the correction pass on PR #25 (19 October 2026), to replace the claim that the notes had "no call, except on a fundamental change".

- Size and coupon: $1.6 billion ($1.84 billion if the underwriters' option is exercised in full); interest accrues from 7 May 2019, payable 15 May and 15 November from 15 November 2019; maturity 15 May 2024.
- Conversion: initially 3.2276 shares per $1,000 (about $309.83 per share). Before 15 February 2024 only on triggers: the closing price at least 130% of the conversion price on at least 20 of the last 30 trading days of the preceding quarter (quarters after 30 September 2019); the trading price below 98% of the stock price times the conversion rate for five consecutive trading days; specified corporate events. From 15 February 2024 to the second scheduled trading day before maturity, at any time.
- Settlement: cash, shares or a combination at Tesla's election; if no timely election, combination settlement with a specified dollar amount of $1,000.
- "We may not redeem the notes prior to the maturity date"; no sinking fund. Description of Notes: "We are not permitted to redeem the notes prior to their maturity."
- Fundamental change: holders may require Tesla to purchase for cash at 100% of principal plus accrued and unpaid interest.
- Make whole fundamental change: the conversion rate is increased for holders converting in connection with it, from a table by effective date and stock price; never above 4.1152 shares per $1,000.

Limits: only the summary and the named Description of Notes sections were read; the make-whole table itself and the full definition of "fundamental change" were not transcribed.
