## Key results

- The saving clause lets the US tax its citizens as if the treaty did not exist, with exceptions.
- Tie-breaker for dual residents: permanent home, centre of vital interests, habitual abode, nationality, then agreement.
- Art. 18 lets contributions to a pension scheme in one state be relieved in the other under conditions.
