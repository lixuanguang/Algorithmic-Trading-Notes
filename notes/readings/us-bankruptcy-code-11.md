## Key results

- A class of claims accepts a plan if holders of at least two-thirds in amount and more than one-half in number of the allowed claims voting accept (1126(c)).
- Cram-down requires the plan to be fair and equitable: for unsecured claims, no junior claim or interest receives or retains property unless the class is paid in full (1129(b)(2)(B)).

## Limits

Statute only; case law, the new-value exception and Subchapter V not covered.
