## Key results

- A class of claims accepts a plan if holders of at least two-thirds in amount and more than one-half in number of the allowed claims voting accept (1126(c)).
- Cram-down requires the plan to be fair and equitable: for unsecured claims, no junior claim or interest receives or retains property unless the class is paid in full (1129(b)(2)(B)).

- Class acceptance (1126(c)) is a voting test within a class; confirmation over a dissenting impaired class needs every other 1129(a) condition except (a)(8), at least one accepting impaired non-insider class (1129(a)(10)), no unfair discrimination and fair and equitable treatment (1129(b)(1)).
- Fair and equitable: secured classes keep liens with deferred payments worth the collateral, sale with liens on proceeds, or the indubitable equivalent (1129(b)(2)(A)); unsecured classes are paid in full or no junior claim or interest receives or retains property on account of it (1129(b)(2)(B)).
- Reinstatement after cure leaves a class unimpaired (1124(2)). A claim is secured only to the value of the collateral (506(a)).
- DIP credit: ordinary-course unsecured credit is an administrative expense (364(a)); superpriority, liens on unencumbered property or junior liens if unsecured credit is unavailable (364(c)); a senior or equal lien only if credit is otherwise unavailable and existing lienholders are adequately protected, the trustee bearing the burden (364(d)). Administrative claims must be paid in cash on the effective date unless the holder agrees otherwise (1129(a)(9)(A)).
