## Key results

- 230-10-45-15(a): payments of dividends or other distributions to owners are financing outflows.
- 230-10-45-16(b): receipts of interest and dividends on loans, debt instruments and equity securities are operating inflows.
- 230-10-45-17(c)-(d): payments to governments for taxes, and to lenders and other creditors for interest, are operating outflows (with the zero-coupon and low-coupon split introduced by the Update).
- 230-10-45-25: the direct method is encouraged; minimum classes of receipts and payments, including interest and dividends received, interest paid and income taxes paid.
- Illustration 230-10-55-10: a direct-method statement with a separate reconciliation of net income to operating cash flow, including an increase in prepaid expenses and a decrease in accounts payable and accrued expenses.
- Effective for public business entities for fiscal years beginning after 15 December 2017; other entities after 15 December 2018, interim periods a year later.

## Limits
