## Key results

- Functional currency normally that of the environment in which cash is generated and expended; 'in principle, a matter of fact' (para. 39).
- A highly inflationary currency (three-year inflation of about 100 percent or more) is not used as a functional currency; the reporting currency is used instead (summary; para. 11).
- Translation adjustments are not included in net income but accumulated in equity (para. 13) and recognised on sale or liquidation (para. 14); transaction gains and losses generally in net income (para. 15).
