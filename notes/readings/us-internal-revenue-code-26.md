## Key results

- Sec. 1014 steps up basis at death; sec. 1015 carries over the donor's basis for gifts; sec. 1091 disallows wash-sale losses within 30 days.
- Sec. 1202 excludes gain on qualified small business stock held long enough, within limits; sec. 1411 imposes a 3.8 percent net investment income tax.
- Sec. 7701(b) defines residence for aliens (substantial presence); secs. 877 and 877A tax covered expatriates; sec. 4942 requires private foundations to distribute about 5 percent of assets.

- Final audit correction pass (2026-10-20): sec. 430 read for single-employer minimum funding: 430(a) minimum required contribution, (c)(1)-(2) installments, (c)(5) zero base when assets reach the funding target, (c)(8) 15-year amortisation for plan years beginning after 31 December 2021, (d)(1) funding target of accrued benefits, (g)(3) asset averaging limits, (h)(2)(C)(iv) corridor of 95-105 percent for 2020-2030.
- PR #33 correction pass (2026-10-21): sec. 430(c)(3), the shortfall amortization base is the funding shortfall minus the present value (at the segment rates) of the shortfall and waiver installments already determined for this and later years on earlier bases; (c)(1) the charge is the aggregate of installments, not less than zero; (c)(6) a zero shortfall zeroes earlier bases; (g)(2)(A) the valuation date is the first day of the plan year; (j)(1)-(3) contributions are due 8.5 months after the plan year, adjusted for interest at the effective rate from the valuation date, with quarterly installments (and 5 further points on late ones) after a shortfall year.
