## Key results

- Effective 1 July 2021 (86 FR 9120).
- Superseded first reading (Batch L3, kept for the record): "applies to ... $100 billion or more in total consolidated assets". The summary's size description is not the applicability rule: under 12 CFR 249.1(b)(1) Category IV institutions are covered only with $50 billion or more of average weighted short-term wholesale funding, and 249.105(b) scales required stable funding at 100, 85 or 70 percent (see ecfr-12cfr249-2026).
