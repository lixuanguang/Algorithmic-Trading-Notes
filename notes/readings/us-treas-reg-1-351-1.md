## Key results

- Para. (c)(1): gain is recognised on a transfer to an investment company if the transfer results in diversification of the transferors' interests and the transferee is a RIC, a REIT, or a corporation more than 80 percent of whose assets (excluding cash and nonconvertible debt) are held for investment and are readily marketable stocks or securities, or interests in RICs or REITs.
- Para. (c)(3): readily marketable = traded on an exchange or regularly quoted over the counter; (c)(5): two or more persons transferring nonidentical assets ordinarily results in diversification; insignificant transfers disregarded; (c)(6): transfers of already diversified portfolios do not count as diversifying.

## Limits

Read through sec. 721(b), which applies the corporate test to partnerships as if incorporated; the partnership regulation (sec. 1.721-1) was not read. Statute sec. 351(e)(1) extends the listed assets.
