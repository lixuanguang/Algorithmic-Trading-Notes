## Key results

- The primary beneficiary of a VIE has (a) the power to direct the activities that most significantly impact its economic performance and (b) the obligation to absorb losses or the right to receive benefits that could be significant; the quantitative expected-losses test was eliminated; reassessment is ongoing; qualifying SPEs are no longer exempt (summary).
- Para. 14A: both power over the most significant activities and significant loss or benefit exposure; only one enterprise can have the power. 14B: power can be contingent on events. 14C: kick-out rights count only if one party can exercise them; protective rights do not preclude power. 14F: design involvement does not alone establish power. Example 1: the special servicer (equity holder, manages defaulted loans) is the primary beneficiary; bondholders absorb losses without power.
