## Key results

- A competitive firm supplies along the rising MC above minimum AVC; shutdown when AVC > p (p. 419); in the long run p >= AC (p. 426); entry drives profit to zero (pp. 433-437).
- Monopoly: MR = MC, p = MC/(1 - 1/|e|), never on inelastic demand (pp. 458-461); natural monopoly when AC still falls at market output (pp. 469-471).
- Monopolistic competition: entry until demand is tangent to AC, excess capacity (pp. 494-497).
- Oligopoly: Stackelberg quantity leadership, price leadership, Cournot (n firms: p(1 - s_i/|e|) = MC), Bertrand (price = MC), collusion and punishment (pp. 516-537).
