## Key results

- Fifteen deep models re-run on FI-2010 and on LOB-2021 (1-15 July 2021) and LOB-2022 (1-15 February 2022), six NASDAQ stocks, ten levels, sampled every ten events, 6-2-2 day splits, labels over k in {1,2,3,5,10} (secs. 3-4.1, pp. 3-5).
- F1 48-61 percent on LOBSTER data against up to 82.6 (BINCTABL) on FI-2010; about half of hyperparameter runs diverged; profitability "far from guaranteed" (sec. 4.4, pp. 6-9).
