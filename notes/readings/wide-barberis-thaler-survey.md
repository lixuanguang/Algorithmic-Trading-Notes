## Key results

- Two building blocks; fundamental risk, noise trader risk and implementation costs (pp. 3-6).
- Beliefs: overconfidence (98 percent intervals contain the truth about 60 percent of the time), optimism, representativeness, conservatism (Edwards: 0.97 against about 0.7), belief perseverance, anchoring, availability; experts not immune (pp. 12-15).
- Preferences: prospect theory, loss aversion, probability weighting, mental accounting and narrow framing, ambiguity aversion (pp. 15-21).
- Anomalies: size, long-term reversal, scaled-price ratios, momentum (pp. 33-34); BSV (pp. 38-39); closed-end fund discount about 10 percent (p. 44); home bias, naive diversification, excessive trading, disposition effect (pp. 47-50).
