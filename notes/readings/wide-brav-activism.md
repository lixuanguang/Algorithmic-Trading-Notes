## Key results

- Activists succeed fully or partly in about two-thirds of 2001-2006 cases, seldom seek control, are mostly non-confrontational; Schedule 13D abnormal returns of 7-8% over (-20,+20) days, not reversed in the following year (pp. 1729-1731).
- Targets are value firms, profitable, with low payout, more defences and higher CEO pay; median holding period about one year (pp. 1730-1731).

## Limits

Sample 2001-2006, U.S.; later evidence not reviewed.
