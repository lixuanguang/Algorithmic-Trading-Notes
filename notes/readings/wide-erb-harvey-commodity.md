## Key results

- Roll return with an unchanged term structure: oil (backwardation, May 2004) printed as 13.1% ($40.95/$36.65 - 1, which is 11.7%); gold (contango) -1.4% (p. 13).
- Heating oil spot 0.9%, roll 4.9%; gold roll -4.8% (p. 14); positive-roll commodities averaged 4.2% excess return against -4.6% (p. 16).
- Diversification return: rebalanced 10.9% against average 7.5% (about 3.4%); half of average variance 12.8 less average covariance 5.3 is 'about 3.5%' (exactly 3.75); not a risk premium (pp. 24-25).

## Limits

Discrepancies: 13.1% against 11.73% from the printed prices; the far contract is dated June 2005 and then July 2005 (p. 13); 3.4% and 3.5% for one diversification return (p. 25).
