## Key results

- Agency MBS carry a government-backed guarantee; non-agency MBS are tranched by seniority (p. 2).
- Median three-month CPR 27.8%, 5th/95th percentiles 2.6% and 52.6% across agency pools (p. 6).
- Covered bonds keep the cover pool on the issuer's balance sheet; prepayments and defaults do not typically reach investors (p. 7).
- Refinancing at par makes MBS callable, so they exhibit negative convexity (p. 10).
