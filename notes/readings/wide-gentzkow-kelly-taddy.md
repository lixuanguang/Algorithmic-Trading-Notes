## Key results

- Text is reduced to a document-term matrix; choices of document unit, tokens, stop words and n-grams matter.
- Dictionary methods suit unlabelled data only if the dictionary fits the domain; penalised regression is a strong default for supervised text.
- Loughran and McDonald's finance-specific word lists outperform general dictionaries on filings.
