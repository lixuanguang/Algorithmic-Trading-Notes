## Key results

- Serial correlation in hedge-fund returns is most likely due to illiquidity exposure and smoothing; a smoothing model with estimators of the smoothing profile and a smoothing-adjusted Sharpe ratio; 908 TASS funds (abstract).
- Proposition 1: smoothing leaves the mean unchanged, scales volatility by sqrt(sum theta_j^2), raises the Sharpe ratio by the inverse, biases the contemporaneous beta toward zero and creates lagged betas theta_m beta; serial correlation sum theta_j theta_(j+m) / sum theta_j^2; smoothing index xi = sum theta_j^2 (pp. 21-23).
