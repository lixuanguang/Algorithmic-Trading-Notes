## Data and timing

- 100 stocks drawn at random, 20 per quintile of trades per day, 1 April to 30 June 2008; 34 million trades and 351 million quotes; DTAQ (millisecond) against MTAQ (second) (sec. III, p. 1758).
- Main-text screens are on quotes and hours: quotes 9:00-16:00 and trades 9:30-16:00, own-venue crossed quotes, the $5 spread rule, non-normal quote conditions (n. 16), zero or missing prices and sizes; NBBO quotes and trades while the NBBO is crossed or locked are deleted (pp. 1750 and 1758).

## Key results

- MTAQ with withdrawn-quote adjustment, Interpolated Time and deletion of crossed or locked states removes the percent quoted spread difference from DTAQ and cuts the percent effective spread and price impact differences by 91 and 97 percent (pp. 1750-1751).
- Interpolated Time, s + (2i-1)/(2I), "uses future information, extending as much as one second into the future" (eq. (16), p. 1766; n. 29, p. 1767).
- DTAQ's Quote Cancel/Correction field was "always blank in our sample" (n. 12, p. 1754).
