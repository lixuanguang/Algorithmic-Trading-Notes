## Data and timing

- Corporate bond ETFs; proprietary AP lists from two issuers, on average 83 percent of passive corporate bond ETF assets, 2004–2015; FINRA TRACE with dealer identifiers; daily creation and redemption baskets (p. 3).

## Key results

- Liquidity mismatch between exchange-traded ETFs and OTC corporate bonds; APs are the only intermediaries that can create and redeem and are "not contractually bound" to arbitrage (p. 1).
- Stylised model: arbitrage effect when APs' bond imbalances are small (sensitivity falls with volatility, illiquidity, trading costs); inventory-management effect when they are large, which can make creations and redemptions run against the mispricing (pp. 2–3).
- Equity ETFs are contrasted as having no liquidity mismatch (p. 2).
