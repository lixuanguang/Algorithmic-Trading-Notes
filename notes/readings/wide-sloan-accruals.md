## Key results

- Sample: 40,679 NYSE/AMEX firm-years 1962-1991, excluding banks and insurers (p. 293); accruals from balance-sheet changes less depreciation (p. 293), scaled by average total assets (p. 294).
- Earnings persistence 0.841; accrual component 0.765 vs cash 0.855 pooled, accrual lower in 86 percent of industries (p. 298; Tables 2-3, pp. 299-300); extreme-accrual firms revert mostly within a year (p. 299).
- Prices act as if investors fixate on earnings; high-accrual firms have negative future abnormal returns around earnings announcements (p. 290).
