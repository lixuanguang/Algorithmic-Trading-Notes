## Key results

- Execution risk in the dark pool sorts informed traders to the exchange; under stated conditions adding a dark pool raises the exchange's signal-to-noise ratio and widens its spread (Propositions 3-4).
- Numerical example: pricing error 7.1 to 2.6 percent at high volatility (p. 770); volume reporting fully reveals value generically (Proposition 5).

## Limits
