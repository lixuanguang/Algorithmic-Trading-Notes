\subsubsection{Equity Market Microstructure}

\begin{principle} \hlt{Equities as Microstructure Laboratory}\\
Equities are the cleanest empirical laboratory because trades, quotes, venue identifiers, and regulatory changes are comparatively well recorded. Lessons from equities transfer to other markets only after adjusting for contract standardisation, post-trade process, leverage, and dealer intermediation.
\end{principle}

\begin{caveat} \hlt{Asset-Class Transfer}\\
An equity-style CLOB is not the default architecture for every asset. Futures, options, FX, fixed income, and crypto differ in tick size, margin, clearing, settlement, inventory risk, and whether liquidity is naturally multilateral or dealer-mediated.
\end{caveat}
